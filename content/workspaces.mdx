# Workspaces

A workspace is one client engagement. It belongs to your accounting firm and can hold **several companies**, which is what a client with more than one legal entity needs. Invoices, bank transactions, master data, categories and settings all live inside a workspace; your firm, its people, billing and templates live above it, in the organization.

Accountants work across many workspaces. Client users see only their own.

## The Workspaces Dashboard

Signing in normally lands you on the **Workspaces** dashboard, your morning overview of where things stand across every client. Not always, though: if you belong to exactly one workspace and are not an organization admin, you are taken straight into it, and if your firm has no workspace yet you get the new-client walk instead of the application.

### Status dots

Each tile carries a coloured dot:

| Dot | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| 🟢 Green | Under control: no open issues, fewer than five items in each bucket |
| 🟡 Amber | Worth a look: a bucket has reached five items, or there is at least one open issue |
| 🔴 Red | Needs attention today: a bucket has fifteen or more items, three or more issues, or there is unreviewed work and no invoice has been uploaded in five days |

### The three counts

- **To review**: bank transactions the AI was not confident enough to categorise.
- **Unmatched**: invoices with no bank transaction against them.
- **Issues**: bank transactions categorised with very low confidence, plus invoices whose extraction failed.

### Sections and search

Tiles are grouped into **Needs attention** (amber or red) and **All caught up** (green), and the count after each heading follows what you search for. The search box filters by workspace name; when nothing matches, the empty state offers **Clear search**.

Below the page header, one line sums up your whole client book: documents to review, unmatched transactions, open issues and how many workspaces are flagged. That line and the greeting above it are written in English whatever language you use the app in.

### Two things that appear on the dashboard

- **An invitation addressed to you** shows in a panel above the banner, so you can accept it without hunting for the email.
- **A client you started setting up and did not finish** shows as a dashed tile with its progress, for example 4/7, and **Continue setup**. Nothing arrives in that workspace until the walk is finished.

## Switching Workspaces

Inside a workspace, the sidebar header is a dropdown: every workspace you belong to, each with its base currency underneath, plus **Back to dashboard**. With a single workspace and no admin rights it is just a label.

## Setting Up a New Client

Only **organization admins** create workspaces. If you need to, ask an admin to promote you (see [Roles and Permissions](/roles-and-permissions)).

**New workspace**, on the dashboard, opens a guided walk of seven screens. Three of them are required and four can be skipped.

<OnboardingStepMapFigure>
The itinerary on the first screen. It is the only place the whole route is visible at once.
</OnboardingStepMapFigure>

1. **Workspace.** Two fields: the **workspace name** and the **country**. The base currency and the time zone follow from the country and are shown, not asked. Both can be changed later.
2. **The first company.** The legal company that owns the bank accounts and appears on the invoices; AI Finance Team uses it to tell income from expense. A Hungarian tax number is read back to you as its core number, EU VAT number and VAT code as you type it, which is the quickest way to catch a typo. If the client has more companies, add them here.
3. **Bank accounts.** Usually skipped: the bank connection is made by the client, in their own browser, after you invite them.
4. **Categories.** A new workspace starts with **no categories**, and nothing is sorted automatically until a list exists. Import your own, or, if your firm keeps a category template, it is applied for you. From the second client onwards you can also copy from another client.
5. **Data sources.** NAV Online Számla, the workspace's own email address for forwarding invoices, and what the client will connect themselves.
6. **Export.** Hungarian workspaces only: the general-ledger numbers the export files need.
7. **People.** Invite the client, and tick the colleagues from your own firm who should be on this engagement. Colleagues are added straight away with the role they already hold; the client is emailed an invitation, offered as **Finance lead** by default. Each row has its own language, so an invitation arrives in the recipient's own. Finish with **I'm done**.

Two things about the walk are worth knowing:

- **The workspace becomes real as you leave screen 3**, not at the end. From there on it exists, it appears on your dashboard, and its sidebar stays locked until you finish the walk. Closing the tab loses nothing.
- **One setup at a time.** Starting another client while one is unfinished takes you back to the unfinished one.

You are an **accountant admin** of every workspace you create. Besides your answers, a new workspace gets a default set of transaction types, the VAT master data it needs, and its own email address for invoices.

## Getting Into an Existing Workspace

The dashboard shows only workspaces you are a member of. Everything else is under **Organization → Manage workspaces**, which lists the firm's workspaces and where you stand with each.

- **Organization admins** pick a role, Accountant or Accountant Admin, and click **Add me**.
- **Everyone else** clicks **Ask to join**, also after picking the role they want. The workspace's admins are emailed, and you are emailed their decision.

Requests are decided **inside the workspace**, under **Settings → Team management → Pending requests**, not in the organization settings.

## Adding People

A workspace's people are managed at **Settings → Team management**, by anyone holding the permission to invite. The panel takes up to ten recipients at a time, each with a name, a role and a language, plus one personal message of up to 500 characters for the whole batch. The pending invitations tab is where you find the link to copy if an email does not arrive.

[Roles and Permissions](/roles-and-permissions) has the full picture: the five roles, who may invite whom, and what each one can see and do.

## Organization Settings

The **Organization** section in the sidebar is open to everyone in your accounting firm: the firm's details, **Manage workspaces**, the firm's team and its invitations, AI access, and support access. Only **Billing** is admin-only.

## Limits

- **A workspace cannot be archived or deleted.** There is no way to do it, by design.
- **There is no limit on how many workspaces you have.** Billing counts documents per month, not workspaces.
- **The closing period is the calendar month.** Each tile's footer names the month being closed; quarterly and other cadences are not supported yet.
