# Subscription & Billing

One subscription covers your whole accounting office: every client workspace and every user, with no limit on either. There is one package. You try it free for 14 days and manage the subscription afterwards on the same page.

The page is under **Organization**, **Billing** in the sidebar. Only an organization admin sees it, and only once AI Finance Team has switched billing on for your office. If you are an admin and the menu item is missing, write to us at founders@aifinance.team and we will switch it on.

## What counts

Billing counts **invoices**. One invoice is one incoming or outgoing invoice of one of your clients, correcting and cancelling invoices included. It counts once, the first time the AI processes it. An invoice that arrives from NAV Online Számla and its PDF copy count as one invoice. Re-running categorization, matching it again or editing it later costs nothing extra.

Bank transactions, bank statements and other documents do not count. They are still processed and shown in the app.

This rests on proportionate use. If, in any month, an office's bank transactions and other documents together come to more than three times its invoices and also more than 1,200, we may offer that office individual pricing. The rule is section 3.8 (Proportionate use) of the Terms of Service.

## The package

There is one package, **Accounting Firm Assistant**: HUF 27,000 + VAT a month for an office in Hungary, EUR 75 + VAT a month for an office outside Hungary. It includes **400 invoices a month**. Above 400, invoices are charged in bands, and each band's rate applies only to the invoices inside that band:

| Invoices in the month | Price per invoice |
| --- | --- |
| The first 400 | Included in the package |
| From 401 to 1,500 | HUF 88 (EUR 0.24) |
| From 1,501 to 3,000 | HUF 81 (EUR 0.23) |
| Above 3,000 | HUF 74 (EUR 0.21) |

For example, an office with 1,501 invoices in a month pays HUF 27,000 for the package, 1,100 × HUF 88 for invoices 401 to 1,500, and HUF 81 for the 1,501st invoice: HUF 123,881 + VAT in all. Nothing stops when you go over 400: no client is cut off.

The package is billed monthly. There is no annual billing. A longer commitment is possible on individual terms: write to us at founders@aifinance.team.

Prices exclude VAT. A Hungarian office is charged 27% Hungarian VAT. An office outside Hungary gives its VAT ID at checkout and is invoiced without VAT under the reverse charge.

The currency follows **your office's country**, not your language: a Hungarian office sees forint prices whatever language its admin uses, every other office sees euro. The full price list is in the [Price List](https://aift.aifinance.team/legal/pricing), part of the Terms of Service.

If we agreed a discount with your office, the page says so under the plan heading (**Agreed discount**). It is applied automatically at checkout; you do not need a code.

## The 14-day free trial

The trial is **once per office**. You give a card at checkout, nothing is charged during the 14 days, and the first payment is taken automatically when the trial ends, unless you cancel before that.

An office that has subscribed before does not get a second trial. For that office the page promises no trial and the button reads **Subscribe**: the first payment is taken at checkout.

If you leave the payment page without finishing, you return to Billing with the note *"Checkout cancelled. Nothing was charged, and you can subscribe whenever you are ready."*

## Billing details for Hungarian offices

A Hungarian office gives its company name and Hungarian tax number in AI Finance Team before the payment page opens. These are the details that go on your invoice.

1. **Company name**: filled in with your office's name the first time, and with the name already on file when you subscribe again. Correct it if your invoices should carry your full legal name.
2. **Tax number (adószám)**: your Hungarian tax number, 11 digits, in the form `12345678-1-23`. Type the digits; the hyphens appear on their own. The number is checked as you type: its 8th digit is a check digit, and the 9th must be between 1 and 5, so a typo is caught here rather than on the invoice.
3. **Continue to payment** stays greyed out until both fields are valid.

### Why the EU VAT number is refused

The field refuses an EU VAT number such as `HU12345678` and says so straight away. The two numbers do different jobs. The **EU VAT number** (közösségi adószám) identifies your company in trade with other EU countries. A Hungarian invoice from a Hungarian supplier to a Hungarian company carries the buyer's **Hungarian tax number** (adószám), so that is the one we need. If only the EU number were on file, your invoice could not be issued correctly.

### The payment page

**Continue to payment** opens Stripe's secure payment page, where you give:

- your **card**;
- your **full billing address**: street, city, postcode and country. The Hungarian invoice must carry the buyer's full address, so the address is required;
- your acceptance of the **Terms of Service**.

The payment page does not ask for the tax number again: the one you gave is already saved. If Stripe does not accept it, you are not sent on; the window shows *"Stripe did not accept this tax number. Check it again, or contact us."*

### Offices outside Hungary

There is no Billing details window. Stripe's payment page asks for the card, the full billing address and your **VAT ID**, which is required, because AI Finance Team sells only to businesses and the VAT ID is what lets us invoice under the reverse charge.

## After you subscribe

The Billing page now has two tabs: **Subscription** and **Invoices**. On **Subscription**, the plan picker gives way to a short summary: **Your subscription**, a status badge (**Trial**, **Active**, **Past due** and so on) and the date that matters next: when the trial ends, when the plan renews, or, after you cancel, when it ends. **Past due** means the last payment did not go through: change the card under **Manage subscription**.

Under it, a usage panel shows the invoices counted **in the current billing period** against the 400 included, what the period is projected to cost with the bands applied, and the split by client. The bank transactions of the period are listed next to the invoices, marked **(not counted)**. A second line gives the calendar month for your own records.

## Managing the subscription

**Manage subscription** opens Stripe's customer portal for your office. There you can:

- change the card;
- see Stripe's payment records and receipts (these are not invoices, see below);
- update the billing details: company name, email, address and phone, and the VAT ID if your office is outside Hungary;
- cancel the subscription.

A cancellation takes effect **at the end of the current billing period**. Until then everything keeps working, and the Billing page shows the end date (**Your subscription cancels on**). Nothing is refunded for the part of the period already started.

Two things to know:

- **Changing the subscription is not possible in the portal.** For a longer commitment on individual terms, write to us at founders@aifinance.team and we will change it for you. Starting a second subscription on the Billing page is refused on purpose, because it would bill you twice.
- **Hungarian offices cannot change the tax number in the portal.** This keeps the Hungarian tax number on your invoices from being replaced by the EU VAT number. If your tax number has changed, write to us.

## Invoices

The **Invoices** tab lists every invoice (számla) AIFT has issued to your office: the supply date, the invoice number, the line and the gross amount. The **PDF** button downloads the invoice from Számlázz.hu. The invoice for a period you have just paid shows **Being issued** until it is ready.

The amount is the amount on the invoice. Számlázz.hu rounds every line to a whole forint, so it can differ by at most 1 forint per line from what was charged to the card.

You also receive each invoice by email. Choose who receives it on the same tab, under **Invoice recipients**.

The documents in the Stripe portal are payment records, not invoices. The invoice to book is the one on the Invoices tab.

## Common questions

**I do not see Billing in the menu.** Either you are not an organization admin (ask one of your admins, or see [Roles & Permissions](/roles-and-permissions)), or billing has not been switched on for your office yet. Write to us.

**The page says "Plans are not available yet."** Your account works and usage is being recorded; the plans for your region are not ready. We will be in touch before anything is charged.

**"You already have a plan covering this work."** Your office already has a subscription. Write to us at founders@aifinance.team to change it rather than starting a second one.

**I typed the tax number and the button stays grey.** Read the red message under the field: it says whether it is the EU VAT number, the format, the check digit or the 9th digit. If you pasted the number, check for a missing digit.
