# Setting Up Payment Approvals

Payment approvals let a client company decide, invoice by invoice, whether a supplier invoice may be paid. This article is for the person who runs the process: the finance admin at the client, or the accountant admin who sets it up for them.

## Switching it on

Open the client workspace, go to **Settings**, then **Approvals**, and switch **Payment approvals are switched on**.

What starts an approval? A supplier invoice enters the flow as soon as it is booked: the extraction and the AI categorisation have finished, it is an expense, it is unpaid, and it is not a duplicate or rejected. The system checks once a minute, so a new invoice reaches its approver within a minute, and approvers see their queue under **Approvals** in the sidebar. Invoices that were already paid before you switched it on are left alone.

Switching it off stops new invoices from entering. Decisions already made stay on the invoices.

## Who does what

Two permissions drive the feature:

- **Approve invoices for payment**: sees the queue and can approve or decline. Needs "View invoices and bank transactions" as well.
- **Manage approvals**: sees the whole flow on the Overview board, assigns invoices, reopens decisions and edits the rules and the policy.

By default the client owner and the accountant admin hold both. For anyone else, create a role under **Settings**, then **Team**, for example "Finance admin" holding both keys, or "Approver only" holding just the first one, and assign it. See [Roles and Permissions](/roles-and-permissions).

## The policy

- **Everything needs approval unless a rule says otherwise**: the safe default. An invoice can only skip approval because of a rule you wrote, never because the AI decided so on its own.
- **Queue window**: a number of days, typed freely. The basis is the invoice due date: approvers see invoices due within this many days, later ones wait until they come closer. An invoice with no due date always appears. Leave it empty to show every open invoice.
- **Invoices flagged "needs review"**: held by the accountant until the extraction is checked, or shown to approvers with a warning.
- **Fallback approver**: the person who receives every invoice no rule places. Without one, such invoices wait in the Unrouted column of the board for you to assign.
- **Bulk approval**: whether approvers may approve several invoices with one confirmation. Declining is always one at a time.
- **Unassigned invoices are visible to every approver**: when off, only people with "Manage approvals" see and decide unrouted invoices; they appear in no list for anyone else.

## Rules

Rules are sentences, checked in order. The first rule that matches an invoice wins.

A rule matches on any combination of partner, invoice category, entity and an amount band in the workspace's base currency. It either sends the invoice to an approver or says no approval is needed. Examples:

- Invoices from Bau-Trans Kft. go to Péter.
- Invoices in Subcontracted work above 500 000 Ft go to Péter.
- Every invoice under 20 000 Ft needs no approval.

Use the arrows to reorder. A rule whose approver lost the permission is skipped and the invoice lands unrouted, so a rule can never leave an invoice with someone who cannot act.

Rule proposals from past decisions and the AI's own routing arrive in a later release. Until then, rules and your own assignments are the whole routing.

## Applying rules to invoices already waiting

A new rule only routes invoices that arrive after it. Anything already waiting with an approver stays there. To make a new or changed rule reach the invoices already waiting, click **Apply rules to waiting invoices** under the rules. The system re-runs the rules over every invoice still waiting and tells you how many it rerouted, how many no longer need approval, and how many stayed unchanged. Invoices someone assigned by hand are not touched.

## What the router learns

Every decision teaches the router. When you assign an invoice to someone by hand, when an approver approves it, or when you mark it as not needing approval, the router remembers the partner and the outcome. Once the last 3 decisions for a partner agree, the next invoice from that partner follows them without a rule: the approver sees "Learned: the last 3 invoices from Hetzner went to you". A "not mine" click forgets that invoice. Declines teach nothing, because a decline is about the invoice, not about who should decide.

## Proposed by AI

At the same moment the router proposes a rule under the rules list, in the same words: "Invoices from Hetzner go to Péter · 3 of 3 past decisions agree". Accept adds it as the last rule, so rules you wrote by hand keep precedence. Dismiss removes it and the same proposal is not made again unless the decisions change. Only a person turns a proposal into a rule. The AI never switches approval off by itself.

## Approver descriptions

Under Approvers, write one or two sentences per person about what they decide: "CEO, signs subcontractors and anything above 500 000 Ft". These descriptions are sent to the AI word for word when an invoice matches no rule and no past decision. The AI picks the person whose description covers the invoice, or nobody when it is not sure; in that case the invoice waits in the Unrouted column with a note like "AI 0.58, below the threshold". Describe what the person decides, not instructions. The AI runs only when at least one approver has a description, and never spends anything for a company where approvals are switched off.

## The Overview board

The **Overview** tab under Approvals shows four columns: **Unrouted** (no rule placed them), **Awaiting** by approver, **Declined** with the approver's note, and **Approved, unpaid**. Overdue invoices carry an amber rail. From a card you can assign an approver, reopen a declined invoice or mark it as not needing approval. The tiles above count what is waiting, what is overdue, and the median time to a decision over the last 30 days.

## What the accountant sees

Every invoice shows its approval state: a pill in the invoice list, a filter value, a "Payment approval" card on the invoice, and each decision on the invoice's history. Approval never blocks exporting or closing a month. It is a cash decision by the client, not a bookkeeping check.

If an accountant rejects an invoice, any pending approval is cancelled and recorded. If the amount or the supplier of an approved invoice changes, the approval is reset and the invoice goes back to its approver.

## Emails

Approvers receive four emails: new invoices to approve (at the earliest 30 minutes after the first one, at most 2 a day), a morning list on working days at 08:00, a due-soon reminder on working days at 15:00 for invoices due within 2 days, and, for finance admins, a declined notice with the approver's note. Each has its own switch on the person's Notifications page. The Friday digest to the accountants gains a line per company with approvals on: waiting, overdue, declined, approved and unpaid. Nothing is sent for a company where approvals are switched off.
