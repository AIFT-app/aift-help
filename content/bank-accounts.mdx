# Connecting Bank Accounts

**Master data → Bank accounts** holds two things: the **account numbers** of your companies, and the **banks you have connected**. Connecting a bank lets AI Finance Team import transactions on its own, so nobody exports a statement by hand.

Account numbers matter even without a connection: incoming transactions are matched against them, which is how a transfer between two of your own accounts is recognised.

## Who Can Do What

| Action | Who |
|---|---|
| Add or edit account numbers | Any member of the workspace |
| Connect a bank | Anyone on the client side (finance lead, finance admin or team member) |
| **Sync now** | Any member, accountants included |
| Disconnect an account, remove a connection | The finance lead only |
| Declare a shared account (cash pool) | An accountant |

Connecting and disconnecting a bank belong to the client side by rule, not by role: no custom role and no exception can grant them to an accountant, because bank credentials must stay with the client. See [Roles and Permissions](/roles-and-permissions).

## Account Numbers

**Bank account numbers**, at the top of the page, lists the IBANs and domestic account numbers of every company, whether or not a bank is connected. Add one with **Add bank account**, choose the **Company**, and give it a **Nickname** if the number alone is not obvious.

Register your numbers before connecting a bank: when the bank returns an IBAN that is already on the list, the company is filled in for you in the next step.

An account that several of your companies use is declared here as well. See [Cash Pool](/cash-pool).

## Connecting a Bank

1. Click **Connect a bank**.
2. Choose the country and the bank. The list is fetched from the provider the first time you open a country, so it can take a moment to fill.
3. Choose **How much history to import**: up to 90, 60 or 30 days. **This cannot be changed later without connecting again**, because your bank fixes the period when it grants the consent. Some banks allow less than 90 days, and the choice is then reduced to what they allow.
4. Click **Continue**. Your bank's own consent page opens, and you sign in there. AI Finance Team never sees your banking credentials, and the access your bank grants is read-only: it cannot move money.
5. The bank's page is in English whatever language you use in AI Finance Team. That is the provider's page, not ours.
6. After you authorise, your browser comes back and the accounts under your consent are listed.

### Choosing which accounts to track

The dialog lists the accounts your bank returned, with the IBAN and the currency:

- Tick the accounts you want to import, and leave the rest.
- Give every ticked account a **company**. **Confirm and start importing** stays disabled until each one has it, so the workspace needs at least one company first.
- Click **Confirm and start importing**.

Two things to expect here. The dialog cannot be closed once it is open, because the consent has already been granted. And it may say "Loading accounts…" for up to half a minute, because the provider is still processing the consent; it retries on its own.

The first import runs straight away and takes a few minutes, depending on how far back you asked it to go.

> **Placeholder names.** If an account arrives as something like "Account (…ab1234)", your bank's API was rate-limiting us when we asked for its details. The account is connected and will sync normally. The name comes from the bank and cannot be edited here.
>
> An account **missing** from the list is a different problem, and not a rate limit. Connect again, and if it stays missing, contact support with the error code shown.

## What Happens After Connecting

- **Transactions arrive twice a day**, in the early morning and the early afternoon, Central European time.
- **The AI gives each transaction a category** and looks for the invoice or partner it belongs to. See [Matching Invoices and Transactions](/invoice-matching).
- **What it is unsure about waits for you** on the Bank transactions and Matching pages rather than being guessed.

You can change which company an account belongs to later: the account's row carries a company list, and client-side members can change it there.

### Sync now

**Sync now** on the connection card fetches immediately, for example after a payment has just cleared. It is limited to **two manual syncs a day per account**. The button does not count them down: when the limit is reached, clicking it says so.

Open banking consent expires. **How long it lasts is your bank's decision**, commonly 90 days, and longer at some banks. The connection card shows the actual date.

Seven days before that date the card turns amber and says **Expires in N days**. Take this seriously: **automatic syncing stops at that moment**, not on the expiry date. The last week is a warning week, not a grace week.

To carry on, connect the same bank again. AI Finance Team recognises it as a renewal, so it does not ask about history again and asks the bank for the longest period it allows. Your existing transactions and everything done with them are kept.

**Mind the gap.** If a consent lapsed before you renewed it, the days between the last sync and the earliest date the provider will now return cannot be recovered from the bank. The card names that period. Fill it from a statement: see [Uploading Bank Statements](/bank-statement-upload).

## Disconnecting

**Disconnect** on an account stops it syncing. The transactions already imported stay in the workspace; nothing is deleted. You can connect the account again at any time.

**Remove** takes a card off the page entirely. It works only on a connection that is disconnected or in error, and only for the finance lead who created it. A connection that has expired cannot be removed, so an old card may stay on the page beside the new one after a renewal.

Both buttons appear only while a connection is active, and only for the finance lead.

## Troubleshooting

**"Your bank was unable to complete the connection."**
The message ends with a code in brackets. It usually means a temporary problem at the bank, so wait a few minutes and try again. If it keeps happening, send support that code: without it the cause cannot be traced.

**No transactions after connecting.**
The first import takes a few minutes. Reload the transactions page, then check the connection card for a sync error and try **Sync now**.

**An account I expected is not in the list.**
That is not a rate limit (those arrive with a placeholder name). Run the connection again.

**The card offers Re-authorize.**
It only tells you to use **Connect a bank**, which is the real way to renew a consent.

**I cannot see Connect a bank.**
It is shown to client-side members only. An accountant cannot connect a client's bank, by design.

**The connection card shows Error.**
Open the card: the status names the provider's reason. A connection in error can be disconnected and connected again by the finance lead.
