# Transaction Notes

You can write notes on a bank transaction the same way as on an invoice. For each note you choose who can see it: only you, your own side (the accounting firm or the client), or everyone who can see the transaction. Nobody receives an email about a note.

## Where to find them

Open the transaction. The **Notes** card stands at the top of the right-hand column of the transaction page, above **Messages**. In a narrower window the right-hand column moves under the transaction's details; **Notes** still comes before **Messages** there.

When the transaction has notes you can see, their number stands next to the **Notes** heading. With none, there is no number.

## Who can see a note

When you write a note, choose in **Who can see it?**:

| Choice | Who sees the note |
|--------|-------------------|
| **A note for myself** | Only you. |
| **Internal note** | Your side: the accountant side if you work at the accounting firm, the client side if you work at the client. The other side does not see it. |
| **Visible to everyone** | Everyone who can see the transaction, on both sides. |

A new note starts as **Internal note**. The line under the field always says what your choice means.

"Your side" is the side the writer belonged to when the note was written.

Everyone who can open the transaction can write notes, on both sides, including a member who sees only the transactions of the statements they uploaded. A note does not change the transaction, so no extra permission is needed.

## Writing a note

1. Open the transaction.
2. Click into the text field of the **Notes** card and type the text, at most 2000 characters. The **Who can see it?** field and the **Add note** button then appear under it.
3. Choose who can see it.
4. Click **Add note** (or press Ctrl+Enter, Cmd+Enter on a Mac).

The note appears at the top of the list, with your name, your role, who can see it, and the date and time. The card shows the three newest notes; when there are more, the **… more notes** button shows the rest.

## Editing and deleting

Only the writer can change a note. On your own note, open the **⋯** menu:

- **Edit** opens the text and the **Who can see it?** field. Click **Save**. When the text changes, the note shows **edited**; a change of who can see it does not.
- **Delete** asks once. A deleted note cannot be restored.

## What notes do not do

- They send no email and no notification. When you need an answer, write on the **Messages** card instead.
- They do not appear in the **History**.
- They never appear in exports and are never sent to Business Central.
- They do not change the transaction's partner, category or matching.

You can write notes on closed transactions too. When the transaction is deleted, its notes are deleted with it. Notes on invoices work the same way; see [Invoice Notes](/invoice-notes).
