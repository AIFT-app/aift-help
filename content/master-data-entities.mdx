# Managing Companies

Companies are your client's own businesses: the legal companies whose invoices and bank accounts this workspace books. Suppliers and customers are partners, not companies (see [Managing Partners](/partners)). Keeping companies accurate in AI Finance Team means the AI can tell which invoices are income and which are expenses, recognise transfers between your own accounts, and reduce the number of exceptions that need manual review.

## What Is a Company?

A company is a record that represents one of your client's own legal companies. Each company in your workspace holds:

- **Legal name** - the official registered name used on documents
- **Country** - the country of registration
- **Tax ID** - the domestic tax identifier (e.g. Hungarian adószám, German Steuernummer)
- **EU VAT number** - the EU-issued VAT number in the standard format (e.g. HU12345678)
- **Registration number** - the company registry number
- **Alternative names** - other names the same party uses on invoices or bank statements (trade names, abbreviations, former names)
- **Bank accounts** - IBAN and/or domestic account numbers used for payments
- **Contact details** - address, website, email domain
- **Matching context** - a free-text note that guides the AI when it encounters ambiguous documents

## Getting to Master Data

From the workspace sidebar, click **Master data**, then **Companies**. This page lists all active companies in your workspace. Archived companies are shown in a separate section below the active list.

### NAV marker next to the tax number

For Hungarian companies, the **Tax ID** column shows a small **NAV** marker when invoice data flows in from NAV Online Számla:

- **Building icon:** the company has its own NAV connection.
- **Group icon:** the company is a paired member of a VAT group (csoportos áfaalany), and the group's NAV connection covers it. Fix or pause that connection on the VAT group, not on the company.
- **Green** means the connection is syncing. **Grey** means it is set up but not syncing right now: paused, not started yet, or the last sync failed. Hover the marker to see which.

No marker means NAV does not cover the company.

## Adding a Company

1. Click **Add company** in the top-right corner.
2. Fill in the **Legal name** (required), **Country** (required), and **Tax ID** (optional).
3. Click **Create company**.

You are taken directly to the company detail page, where you can add all remaining fields: EU VAT number, registration number, alternative names, bank accounts, contact details, and matching context. There is no need to enter everything at once - fill in what you have now and return later as you learn more.

> **One company per tax number.** A workspace holds each company once. As you type the Tax ID, AI Finance Team checks it against the companies you already have, in any spelling (`12345678-2-41`, `12345678` and `HU12345678` are the same company). If it already exists, the form names it and links to it, and **Create company** stays disabled. Archived companies do not count. A VAT group identifier is never treated as a company's own number.

## Editing a Company

Open the company by clicking its name in the list. Each section of the detail page can be edited and saved independently.

### Identity

Change the legal name, country, Tax ID, EU VAT number, or registration number. Click **Save** when done. If the new Tax ID already belongs to another company in the workspace, the form names that company and **Save** stays disabled.

#### How the Tax ID and EU VAT number relate

A company can hold two tax identifiers: a **domestic Tax ID** (the local tax number - e.g. the Hungarian adószám or the German Steuernummer) and an **EU VAT number** (the cross-border VAT identifier - e.g. `HU12345678`, `DE123456789`). How AI Finance Team treats the pair depends on the country:

- **Countries where the two share the same number** - Hungary, Poland, Romania, Spain, Portugal, and most other EU members. Here the EU VAT number is just the domestic number with a country prefix, so AI Finance Team cross-checks them: when both are present and consistent you see a small ✓, and if they don't line up a soft amber warning appears so you can double-check. The warning never blocks saving.
- **Countries where the two are separate numbers** - Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, France, and a few others. The Steuernummer / UID and the EU VAT number are independent identifiers that cannot be derived from one another, so AI Finance Team validates each on its own and does **not** compare them. You will **not** see a "they don't share the same number" warning for a correct German or Austrian pair - a short note under the EU VAT field reminds you the two are checked separately.
- **Non-EU companies** - e.g. Switzerland, the United Kingdom, the United States. Only the domestic Tax ID applies, so the EU VAT number field is hidden.

Both identifiers help recognise the company on incoming documents: an invoice that shows **either** the domestic Tax ID or the EU VAT number will still identify the company correctly - and for your own companies, book the document as income or expense in the right direction.

### Alternative Names

Add any other names this party uses - for example, a trading name that appears on their invoices, an abbreviation used in bank references, or a former company name after a rebrand.

To add a name, type it into the field and click **Add** (or press Enter). To remove one, click the × next to it.

These names are used during document matching: when the AI encounters a name it doesn't recognise, it checks it against the alternative names of all companies in your workspace.

### Bank Accounts

Add the IBAN and/or domestic account numbers associated with this company. For Hungarian companies, you can store both the international IBAN and the domestic account number (the three-segment format).

To add an account, fill in the fields and click **Add account**. To remove one, click the × next to it.

Bank accounts are used during transaction matching: incoming or outgoing payments are linked to the company whose account number appears in the bank statement reference.

### Contact Details

Store the company's address, website, and email domain. The email domain is particularly useful for companies whose invoices arrive by email - when the AI sees a message from `@acmesolutions.com`, it can associate it with the correct company.

### Matching Context

This is a short free-text note for the AI - not customer-facing, not displayed on any document. Use it to resolve cases where automatic matching is ambiguous:

- Distinguish two companies with similar names ("WKA Studio is the agency; WKA Holding is the parent company - keep separate")
- Note a known quirk in how this party labels their invoices ("always uses 'WKA Creative' on invoice headers despite legal name being WKA Studio Kft.")
- Flag a party that issues invoices in a different language than expected

The note is used as additional context during AI categorisation. A few sentences are usually enough.

### Representatives

Assign workspace members as the primary contact for this company. Representatives appear on the company detail page and are used in internal workflows to route questions or exceptions to the right person.

To assign a representative, click **+ Add** and select a workspace member. To remove one, click the × next to their name.

## Archiving and Restoring a Company

If a company is no longer active - a supplier relationship has ended, a client has been wound down, or a duplicate was created by mistake - you can archive it rather than delete it.

Archived companies:
- Are hidden from the active company list and from the company picker when categorising documents
- Are preserved in the database, so historical documents linked to them remain intact
- Can be fully restored at any time

To archive, open the company, scroll to the **Danger zone** section, and click **Archive company**.

To restore, scroll down to the **Archived companies** section on the companies list page and click **Restore**. If another company took the same tax number while this one was archived, restoring is refused and the message names that company.

## Permanently Deleting a Company

Permanent deletion removes the company record and is irreversible. It is only available for companies that have no linked documents (invoices, transactions). If there are linked documents, you will need to archive instead.

To delete, open an archived company, scroll to the **Danger zone**, and click **Delete permanently**.

## Why Company Data Quality Matters

The AI categorisation engine uses company data as one of its strongest signals. When a company record is complete:

- Partner identification is faster and more accurate
- Alternative names catch document variants that would otherwise generate unmatched exceptions
- Bank accounts enable automatic payment matching
- Matching context resolves edge cases without manual intervention

Incomplete company records are one of the most common reasons AI confidence scores stay low. If you notice the AI repeatedly misidentifying a partner, the first thing to check is whether that party exists as a company and whether its alternative names are up to date.
