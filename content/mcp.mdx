# Connecting an AI assistant via MCP

AI Finance Team has a built-in **MCP server**, a standard interface that lets AI assistants such as Claude, Mistral Le Chat or ChatGPT read your bookkeeping data, and with your permission change it. It is the same product, reached by conversation.

Once connected, you can ask in plain language ("Which invoices in Quillmoor Software need my attention?") and the assistant pulls live data from your workspace.

The article is written for accountants, who can use every tool. A client-side member can connect too and read their own workspace, but the tools that change something will refuse them.

## Before You Connect

**AI assistant access must be switched on for your accounting firm.** Open **Organization → AI access**. Every member of the firm can see whether it is on; an **organization admin** can change it.

A few things worth knowing about that switch:

- It covers the whole organization and every client workspace in it. The web app is unaffected either way.
- Turning it off takes effect immediately, including for assistants that are already connected. Turning it back on restores them without reconnecting.
- The page also states which AI providers receive data when you use this, and is the right place to read that before you connect.
- AI Finance Team can also switch it off, with a reason. Your organization can override that itself on the same page.

## What It Can Do

The connection sits on the same data and the same permissions you already have. It cannot see what you cannot see, and it can only do what you could do in the app. Every call is recorded.

### Questions (nothing changes)

> "List my client workspaces and tell me which need the most attention."

> "In Slatebridge Roofing, which bank transactions in May have no matching invoice yet?"

> "Show me Q1 for Quillmoor Software, the P&L and the cash-flow report."

> "Which Gearmont Fleet Services invoices have uncategorised lines, largest first?"

> "Find every card payment in Reamwell Office Supplies from March."

> "What has been done in this workspace through MCP today?"

### Actions (the Make changes scope)

> "Mark the bank fees in May as no invoice needed."

> "Categorise this subscription invoice line as software licence."

> "Confirm the suggested match between AH-INV-2026-0314 and AH-TXN-2026-0892."

> "This invoice's partner is wrong: set it to the verified partner Slatebridge Roofing."

> "Promote this supplier to a verified partner."

> "Undo the categorisation on AH-TXN-2026-0042."

Categorising and editing invoices are **accountant-only**, whichever scope you granted.

## What It Cannot Do

- **Move money.** No transfers, no payments, no access to bank credentials.
- **Reach your credentials.** Bank connection settings, passwords and tokens are all out of reach. The assistant can see *whether* a NAV connection exists, never its credentials.
- **Upload or re-read a document.** Files go through the app or your workspace's email address. The assistant cannot take an attachment, and it cannot ask for a document to be read again.
- **Export to your accounting system.** Exports stay a deliberate step in the app.
- **Touch other workspaces.** It sees only workspaces you belong to, and only while the firm's AI access is on.

These are enforced on our side. Asking the assistant to do them anyway changes nothing.

## Setting Up the Connection

The URL is always the same:

```
https://aift.aifinance.team/api/mcp
```

### Claude (desktop or web)

Available on Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise plans.

1. Open **Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector**.
2. Paste the URL.
3. Click **Add**. Claude opens an AI Finance Team sign-in tab.
4. Sign in with your normal account.
5. On the consent screen, tick **Read your books**, and **Make changes** as well if you want the assistant to be able to categorise, match and edit.
6. Click **Allow**.

To disconnect: Settings → Connectors → AI Finance Team → **Remove**.

### Mistral Le Chat

Available on Free and Pro.

1. **Settings → MCP Connectors → Add Connector**.
2. Paste the URL. The sign-in is detected automatically.
3. Sign in and give consent as above.

### ChatGPT

Available on Plus and Pro through Developer Mode, or published by your organization on Business and Enterprise.

1. Enable **Developer Mode** in settings.
2. **Add Custom Connector** with the URL above.
3. Sign in and give consent as above.

ChatGPT is given two extra general-purpose tools of its own shape, so its answers can look different from Claude's on the same question.

### Microsoft Copilot and Gemini Enterprise

Possible through Copilot Studio or Gemini Enterprise, and both need your IT team. Ask us if you want help.

## Read and Make Changes

The consent screen has two tick boxes.

| Scope | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Read your books** | Every read tool: your workspaces, the ledger, invoices and transactions, master data, reports, missing invoices, the NAV list, problem invoices, comments, the workspace's and the firm's member list, and your own MCP activity. |
| **Make changes** | Mark and unmark "no invoice needed", categorise invoices and transactions, confirm and undo matches, edit an invoice's direction, partner or company, and link or verify a partner. |

**Always tick Read**, even when you want the assistant to make changes: a write-only connection is possible and useless, because every question fails.

Two details about reading that are easy to miss: the member lists include colleagues' **names and email addresses**, and an invoice comes back with its payment-approval decision, the payee's bank account and its confirmation state, and the payment file it went into.

"Master data" is one tool covering ten kinds: companies, partners, bank accounts, categories, transaction types, custom category lists and their values, counterparties the workspace has not verified yet, the NAV connection status and the Business Central pairings.

Disconnect and connect again to change what you granted.

## When It Does Not Work

The assistant shows the message it received. Each one means something different:

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| AI assistant (MCP) access is turned off for your organization | An organization admin turns it on under **Organization → AI access**. |
| Your account does not belong to an organization that can use MCP | Contact us. This is a setup gap, not the switch. |
| Your sign-in for this connection is no longer valid | Disconnect the connector and add it again. |
| Access could not be verified, try again shortly | A temporary fault. Try again, and tell us the time if it persists. |

Two more cases have no message of their own:

- **Everything is empty.** If your role or an exception has taken "View invoices and bank transactions" away from you, every read tool returns nothing while the connection still looks healthy. See [Roles and Permissions](/roles-and-permissions).
- **You are asked to sign in again after a long break.** A connection left untouched for about a month has to be re-authorised. This is normal.

## What Is Recorded

Every call writes one row to the audit log: who made it, which tool, when, how long it took, how many records came back, and whether it failed. For a change, the record that was touched and its values before and after are stored as well.

Two limits are worth understanding:

- **The arguments are recorded selectively.** Names of the expected arguments are kept; anything else, including any long or free-text value, is replaced. Search terms are not stored.
- **Results are never stored.** The log holds the shape of the call, not the data the assistant saw.

You can ask the assistant what it has done: "What have I done through MCP in the last hour?" That lists the calls, not the before and after values. If a change went wrong, the fix is to make the right change: categorise it again with the correct value, or set the field back. For anything you cannot repair that way, ask us and we will read the log.

## Tips

- **Name the workspace.** "In Quillmoor Software…" beats "at my client's", because the assistant can see all of your workspaces.
- **Use the visible identifiers.** Invoice numbers like `AH-INV-2026-0042` and transaction numbers like `AH-TXN-2026-0157` are shown in the app and the assistant can resolve them directly.
- **Follow the links it gives you.** Invoices and transactions come back with a link straight into the app. Other records do not; ask the assistant to fetch the invoice or the transaction, and you get one.
- **One person, one connection.** A connector is tied to your account. Each colleague sets up their own, and every action is stamped with who performed it.

## Common Questions

**Does the assistant keep my data?**
We return data per request. Whether the assistant keeps anything afterwards is a matter of its provider's policy and your settings there. On our side we keep the log of which calls were made, not what they returned.

**Can the assistant do something I am not allowed to do?**
No. Every tool re-checks your permissions, and the ones that change data also require an accountant role.

**Can I use it on my phone?**
Yes, wherever the assistant runs. The connection belongs to your account, not to a device.

**Can it export to my accounting system?**
No. Exports to Kulcs-Soft, Novitax, DATEV or BMD stay in the app.

## Need Help?

Tell us the workspace, the assistant you use and roughly when it happened. That is usually enough to find the call in the log and see what went wrong.
