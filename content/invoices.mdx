# Working with Invoices

AI Finance Team reads every invoice for you: it extracts the data, works out which of your companies the invoice belongs to and whether it is income or an expense, finds the partner, and suggests categories and VAT codes. This article explains how invoices arrive, how to find and check them, and how to correct, reject and download them.

## How invoices arrive

- **Upload**: on the **Invoices** page, click **Upload invoices** in the top-right corner, or drop files anywhere on the page: PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC, up to 50 MB each, or a `.zip` archive, which the browser unpacks and queues file by file. A progress panel in the bottom-right corner follows each file. Once a file has uploaded, its extraction keeps going even if you leave or reload the page; a file that is still uploading from your browser is lost on reload. See [Uploading Files](/uploading-files).
- **Email**: forward invoices to the workspace's address. See [Forwarding Documents by Email](/email-forwarding).
- **NAV Online Számla** (Hungary): invoices reported to NAV arrive by themselves, without a PDF. See [NAV Online Számla](/nav-online-szamla).

### What happens next

Claude reads the invoice and extracts the header, the parties, the amounts and the line items. AI Finance Team then works out which of your companies is on the invoice: if your company is the seller, the invoice is income; if it is the buyer, it is an expense. It finds the partner, suggests a category for each line and the VAT codes, and writes a one-line summary. Categories are suggested once the direction is known. See [Categorising Invoices & Transactions](/categorization) and [VAT Codes on Invoices](/vat-codes-on-invoices).

### Duplicates

When you upload a file identical to one already in the workspace, AI Finance Team creates the invoice but marks it as a duplicate of the original and does not extract it again. The upload panel shows **Already exists**, and a click opens the original. After extraction, an invoice with the same invoice number, issue date, gross amount and currency as another one is marked a duplicate too.

Duplicates are hidden from the list: they appear as a **related** row you can expand under the original, and with the **Duplicate** filter on. If two invoices really are different (a supplier reused a PDF for two bills), open the duplicate and click **Mark as not a duplicate** on its banner.

## The invoice list

1. **Partner**, with the invoice number underneath. A click anywhere on the row opens the invoice.
2. **Labels**: up to three pills that say what the invoice needs, then **+N** for more.
3. **Amount**: the gross total in the invoice currency, green with an up arrow for income, red with a down arrow for an expense.

Click **Invoices** in the sidebar. The list has these columns: **ID** (AI Finance Team's own number, when your workspace shows it; click to copy), **Date** (the issue date), **Partner**, **Labels** and **Amount**. It shows 50 invoices per page and can be sorted by ID, date or amount. An amber left edge marks an invoice that needs action.

The labels you meet most often:

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Review** | The extraction is incomplete or failed: check the invoice against the document |
| **Company needed** | AI Finance Team could not tell which of your companies the invoice belongs to |
| **Pick direction** | Income or expense could not be decided; choose it on the invoice |
| **NAV** / **No PDF** | The invoice came from NAV Online Számla / there is no document file |
| **FX pending** | The exchange rate to your base currency is not available yet |
| **Unverified** | The partner is not a verified partner yet (see [Managing Partners](/partners)) |
| **VAT codes incomplete** / **Default-coded** | Lines without a VAT code / lines coded with the default code |
| **Duplicate**, **Cancelled** | A duplicate of another invoice / cancelled by a storno invoice |
| **Awaiting approval**, **Approved**, **Declined** | The payment approval state, where approvals are switched on (see [Approving Invoices for Payment](/approving-invoices)) |

### Finding invoices

Search by invoice number, AI Finance Team ID, seller or buyer name, or partner. Above the list you can filter by date range, **Company** (when you have more than one), **Direction**, **Source** (NAV or uploaded), **Payment** and **Approval**, and switch on filter pills: **Needs review**, **Unpaid**, **Company needed**, **FX pending**, **Duplicate**, **Rejected**, **No document**, **VAT codes incomplete** and **VAT: default-coded**. Rejected invoices, duplicates and cancelled storno pairs stay hidden unless their pill is on. If you work in a [focused period](/focused-period), the list follows it.

With permission to edit invoices you can tick rows and **Re-categorize** them, **Re-run VAT coding**, or **Approve reviewed items**.

## The invoice page

Click an invoice to open its page. The top card shows the invoice number, badges such as **Duplicate**, **Rejected**, **Needs review** and **Completeness**, the payment status, and the facts **Partner**, **Direction**, **Gross** and **Issued**, with the **Download** and **Edit** buttons. Under it are the AI summary, which you can edit, and the **To-do** chips, for example a missing VAT code or a partner to verify.

The page has six tabs:

- **Overview**: the **Parties** (seller and buyer, with your side marked), **Amounts** (net, VAT and gross, with the base-currency amounts where they differ) and **Dates** (issue, fulfilment, due, VAT and posting date), plus custom categories and the payment approval card where they apply.
- **Lines & VAT**: the line items with description and quantity, net, VAT rate, gross, category and VAT code.
- **Document**: the original file, or the NAV rendering for a NAV invoice.
- **Matching & payment**: the bank transactions matched to the invoice. See [Invoice Matching](/invoice-matching).
- **Messages**: the conversation about the invoice. See [Messages](/messages).
- **History**: every change on a timeline, field by field with the old and the new value, who made it and when, together with extraction, matching and NAV events. It can be filtered by person, and it cannot be edited.

Some things are corrected right on the page, such as the summary, the VAT and posting dates, the categories and the VAT codes.

## Editing an invoice

Click **Edit** on the invoice page, or press `E` when no field is focused. You need the permission to edit invoices. The editor opens full screen, with the document on the left (on wide screens) and the form on the right:

- **Assignment**: whether you are the seller or the buyer (**We are the seller · income** or **We are the buyer · expense**), which of your companies it is, and the partner. The names and tax numbers printed on the invoice are not edited: you choose who the invoice belongs to.
- **Invoice details**: the invoice number, the currency, and the issue, due, fulfilment, VAT and posting dates.
- The net, VAT and gross totals.
- **Line items**: the description, net, gross and category of each line can be edited; the VAT rate is shown but not edited. **Add row** adds a line, and the bin icon removes one.

Click a value to edit it; it is saved when you press Enter or click away. There is no Save button. When you hover over a field, the value is highlighted in yellow in the PDF, so you can check it against the source. A field you changed shows a revert arrow on hover, which restores the value the AI extracted. Press `Esc` or click **×** to close the editor.

## Rejecting an invoice

If an invoice does not belong to any of your companies, open the company choice on the invoice and click **This invoice isn't for any of our companies**, then give a reason. The invoice moves to the **Rejected** filter, matching suggestions for it are discarded, and existing matches are kept and flagged. Its banner offers **Undo rejection**. Invoices cannot be deleted.

## Downloading an invoice

Click **Download** on the invoice page to get the original file, or the **▾** arrow to choose a format:

| Format | Contents |
|--------|----------|
| **PDF** | The original document |
| **XLSX** | Two sheets: "Invoice" with the summary and "Line Items" with every line |
| **CSV** | The invoice header on the first rows, then the line items |
| **JSON** | All invoice fields and a `line_items` list |

The files are named after the original file. XLSX, CSV and JSON are built from the current data, so they include your corrections; their column headers are in English. An invoice without a document file (for example from NAV) has no Download button.

## Extraction and review

The upload panel shows each file as **Queued**, **Uploading…**, **Extracting…**, **Extracted** or **Failed**, with **Retry** for a failed one. While an invoice is still being read, its page shows the extraction state in the top card.

The **Review** label means the extraction scored below 80% for completeness: the invoice number, a date, a tax number, an amount or the line items are missing, or the totals do not add up. The invoice page shows the score as **Completeness: N%**. A failed extraction is labelled **Review** too. Check such invoices against the document and correct them in the editor.

## Who can do what

Seeing invoices needs the permission to view invoices and bank transactions. Editing, rejecting and the bulk actions need the permission to edit invoices, which accountants hold by default; client users can view invoices, open the document and upload. See [Roles and Permissions](/roles-and-permissions).

## Tips

- **Suppliers and customers are partners.** If a supplier is not recognised, add or verify it under Master data, Partners. **Companies** are your own businesses. See [Managing Partners](/partners).
- **Use the History tab** to see who changed what and when.
- **Download as XLSX** to share an invoice with a colleague who works in Excel: the summary and the lines stay on separate sheets.
