# Working with Invoices

AI Finance Team reads every invoice for you: it extracts the data, works out which of your companies the invoice belongs to and whether it is income or an expense, finds the partner, and suggests categories and VAT codes. This article explains how invoices arrive, how to find and check them, and how to correct, archive and download them.

## How invoices arrive

- **Upload**: on the **Invoices** page, click **Upload invoices** in the top-right corner, or drop files anywhere on the page: PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC (iPhone photos, converted to JPEG before upload), up to 50 MB each, or a `.zip` archive, which the browser unpacks and queues file by file. A progress panel in the bottom-right corner follows each file. Once a file has uploaded, its extraction keeps going even if you leave or reload the page; a file that is still uploading from your browser is lost on reload. See [Uploading Files](/uploading-files).
- **Email**: forward invoices to the workspace's address. See [Forwarding Documents by Email](/email-forwarding).
- **NAV Online Számla** (Hungary): invoices reported to NAV arrive by themselves, without a PDF. See [NAV Online Számla](/nav-online-szamla).

### What happens next

Claude reads the invoice and extracts the header, the parties, the amounts and the line items. AI Finance Team then works out which of your companies is on the invoice: if your company is the seller, the invoice is income; if it is the buyer, it is an expense. It finds the partner, suggests a category for each line and the VAT codes, and writes a one-line summary. Categories are suggested once the direction is known. See [Categorising Invoices & Transactions](/categorization) and [VAT Codes on Invoices](/vat-codes-on-invoices).

### Duplicates

When you upload a file identical to one already in the workspace, AI Finance Team creates the invoice but marks it as a duplicate of the original and does not extract it again. The upload panel shows **Already exists**, and a click opens the original. After extraction, an invoice with the same invoice number, issue date, gross amount and currency as another one is marked a duplicate too.

Duplicates are hidden from the list: they appear as a **related** row you can expand under the original, and under **Record state: Duplicate**. If two invoices really are different (a supplier reused a PDF for two bills), open the duplicate and click **Mark as not a duplicate** on its banner.

## The invoice list

The invoice list with four invoices. The numbers match the list below. The companies and amounts are examples.
</InvoiceListFigure>

1. **Date**, with AI Finance Team's own ID underneath when your workspace shows IDs. Click the ID to copy it.
2. **Partner**, with the invoice number underneath. A long name wraps onto a second line. A click anywhere on the row opens the invoice.
3. **Labels**: what the invoice needs comes first, where it came from (**NAV**, **No document**) last. Up to three pills show, and **+N** stands for the rest: never a label that needs action, and pointing at it lists what it stands for.
4. **Amount**: the gross total in the invoice currency, green with an up arrow for income, red with a down arrow for an expense.

Click **Invoices** in the sidebar. The list has these columns: **Date** (the issue date) with the **ID** under it (AI Finance Team's own number, when your workspace shows it; click to copy), **Partner**, **Labels**, **Company** (only when your workspace has more than one company: which of your companies the invoice belongs to, or a dash if AI Finance Team has not assigned it yet) and **Amount**. It shows 50 invoices per page and can be sorted by date, by ID (the second line of the date header) or by amount. An amber left edge marks an invoice that needs action. The list uses the full width of the window. In a narrow window it scrolls sideways, and the **Amount** column stays in view on the right.

The labels you meet most often:

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Reading failed** / **Check the extracted data** | The document could not be read / the extracted data scored low: check it against the document |
| **Company needed** | AI Finance Team could not tell which of your companies the invoice belongs to |
| **Pick direction** | Income or expense could not be decided; choose it on the invoice |
| **Exchange rate missing** | The rate to your base currency could not be fetched; enter it on the invoice |
| **Unverified partner** | The partner is not a verified partner yet (see [Managing Partners](/partners)) |
| **Incomplete categorization** | Lines without a category, including lines where the AI's suggestion waits for you |
| **VAT codes incomplete** / **VAT coded by default** | Lines without a VAT code / lines coded with the default code |
| **Line total differs from the header** | The line items do not add up to the invoice's totals |
| **Overdue** | Unpaid after its due date. An unpaid invoice that is not yet due is no to-do |
| **Match suggestion awaiting a decision** | AI Finance Team suggests a bank transaction for it; confirm or reject it |
| **NAV** / **No document** | The invoice came from NAV Online Számla / there is no document file. Amber where your workspace expects documents, grey elsewhere |
| **Duplicate**, **Cancelled** | A duplicate of another invoice / cancelled by a storno invoice |
| **Awaiting payment approval**, **Approved**, **Declined** | The payment approval state, where approvals are switched on (see [Approving Invoices for Payment](/approving-invoices)) |
| **To file**, **To book** | A checkpoint still waiting, where filing or bookkeeping approval is switched on (see [Filing and Bookkeeping Approval](/filing-and-bookkeeping-approval)) |
| **Sent back to filing** | The bookkeeper sent the invoice back to filing; point at the label to read the note |

### Finding invoices

Search by invoice number, AI Finance Team ID, seller or buyer name, or partner. Above the list you can filter by date range, **Company** (when you have more than one), **Direction**, **Source** (NAV or uploaded), **Record state**, **Processing**, **Coding**, **Payment**, **Partner** and **Document**. Each dropdown offers every value of its own dimension and nothing from another one, and a step your workspace has switched off has no dropdown and no value. **Record state** opens on **Active**: archived invoices, duplicates and cancelled storno pairs stay hidden until you pick **Recently archived** (the last 30 days), **All archived**, **Duplicate** or **Cancelled**. Under **Payment**, **Overdue** lists unpaid invoices past their due date.

Where an approval is switched on, the **Workflow** filter groups its values by step: **Filing**, **Bookkeeping** and **Payment approval**. Within a group any of the values you pick will do (or); across groups every one must hold (and), and the filter bar spells the condition out, for example: Filing: approved AND Bookkeeping: approved. With at least two steps on, **Approved in every step that is on** picks the invoices approved in each of them in one click. **No approval needed** lists the invoices that need no payment approval; the invoice says why. With payment approval on, the **Approver** filter narrows to a person: a colleague who is the approver of at least one invoice, yourself (**Me**), or **No approver assigned**. Together with **Payment approval: Awaiting approval** it shows what waits on someone.

The pills above the list are the to-dos. Each to-do pill uses the same words as the label it filters, and shows how many invoices carry that to-do under your other filters; a to-do with nothing to count has no pill, and a step your workspace has switched off has none either. If you work in a [focused period](/focused-period), the list follows it. A link saved before these filters existed still opens the same invoices.

With permission to edit invoices you can tick rows and **Re-categorize** them, **Re-run VAT coding**, or **Approve reviewed items**.

Some to-dos are right as they are: the extracted data was checked, a line total differs on purpose, no document will come, the default VAT code is correct. The invoice's page then shows **Accept to-dos**: tick what is right, add a note if you like, and click **Accept**. The to-do goes, and the invoice keeps who accepted it, when, and the note. If the data changes afterwards, the to-do comes back. Where filing or bookkeeping approval is waiting on the invoice, that approval accepts these to-dos instead, and the **Approvals** card says which ones.

## The invoice page

Click an invoice to open its page. The top card shows the invoice number, badges such as **Duplicate**, **Archived**, **Needs review** and **Completeness**, the payment status, and the facts **AIFT id** (AI Finance Team's own number for the invoice, always shown here; click to copy), **Partner**, **Direction**, **Gross** and **Issued**, with the **Download** and **Edit** buttons. Under it are the AI summary, which you can edit, and the **To-do** chips, for example a missing VAT code or a partner to verify.

The page has seven tabs:

- **Overview**: the **Parties** (seller and buyer, with your side marked), **Amounts** (net, VAT and gross, with the base-currency amounts where they differ) and **Dates** (issue, fulfilment, due, VAT and posting date), plus custom categories where they apply. Above the payment approval card sits an **Approvals** card listing the checkpoints this invoice passes - filing, bookkeeping and payment - each with its state and who decided it. It appears even where only payment approvals are switched on. See [Filing and Bookkeeping Approval](/filing-and-bookkeeping-approval).
  - On the **Parties** card, clicking the partner's or your own company's tile (it shows **Details**) opens a side panel. For a partner you see the tax numbers, the bank accounts with their confirmation, the company register result and the rules set on it; for your company its identity, its bank accounts and the state of its NAV connection. The panel is read-only, so you can use it even if you cannot open the partner page. **Edit on the partner page** opens that page in the same tab, and its **Back to invoice** brings you back here, also after you switch tabs there. A partner that is not verified yet has no panel: its **Verify partner** button opens the partner picker instead.
- **Lines & VAT**: the line items with description and quantity, net, VAT rate, gross, category and VAT code. Where the workspace has [custom category lists](/custom-categories), each line's custom categories sit under the line and are set here.
- **Document**: the original file, or the NAV rendering for a NAV invoice. The small icon right after the tab's name opens the same document in a separate window, from any tab, so it can stay open beside the invoice, for example on a second screen. The next invoice's document loads into that same window. An invoice that has no file yet, for example one that arrived from NAV, offers **Attach the invoice PDF** here: the file goes straight onto this invoice, nothing is extracted and nothing is re-filed. Afterwards **Run extraction** can read from the document what the NAV data lacks, such as the buyer's address, the bank account or the payment method; NAV's amounts, dates and tax numbers never change. This is the invoice's own document, not an attachment: supporting papers belong on the Attachments tab. If the file is wrong, for example an unreadable scan or another invoice's document, **Archive document** takes it off the invoice with a reason; you choose whether to archive only the document or also withdraw the data the AI read from it, and the dialog lists the fields that will be emptied before you confirm. NAV's data never changes. Under the viewer, **Archived documents** lists every file the invoice used to carry, with the reason, and **Restore** puts a file back as long as the invoice has no other document.
- **Attachments**: supporting documents such as a performance certificate, a timesheet or a delivery note, to preview, download or add. See [Invoice Attachments](/invoice-attachments).
- **Matching & payment**: the bank transactions matched to the invoice. See [Invoice Matching](/invoice-matching).
- **Messages**: the conversation about the invoice. See [Messages](/messages).
- **History**: every change on a timeline, field by field with the old and the new value, who made it and when, together with extraction, matching and NAV events, the sends to Business Central and the HessynKettős exports. It can be filtered by person, and it cannot be edited.

Some things are corrected right on the page, such as the summary, the VAT and posting dates, the categories and the VAT codes.

## Editing an invoice

Click **Edit** on the invoice page, or press `E` when no field is focused. You need the permission to edit invoices. The editor opens full screen, with the document on the left (on wide screens) and the form on the right:

- **Assignment**: whether you are the seller or the buyer (**We are the seller · income** or **We are the buyer · expense**), which of your companies it is, and the partner. The names and tax numbers printed on the invoice are not edited: you choose who the invoice belongs to.
- **Invoice details**: the invoice number, the currency, and the issue, due, fulfilment, VAT and posting dates.
- The net, VAT and gross totals.
- **Line items**: the description, net, gross and category of each line can be edited; the VAT rate is shown but not edited. You pick the category from a searchable list: type a name or a code to filter. Categories are listed by code, ascending, and the AI's suggestion, if there is one, sits at the top. **Add row** adds a line, and the bin icon removes one.

Click a value to edit it; it is saved when you press Enter or click away. There is no Save button. When you hover over a field, the value is highlighted in yellow in the PDF, so you can check it against the source. A field you changed shows a revert arrow on hover, which restores the value the AI extracted. Press `Esc` or click **×** to close the editor.

## Archiving an invoice

An invoice that should not be in the books, because it is private, was sent to you by mistake or belongs to none of your companies, is **archived**, never deleted. Click **Archive** in the top card of the invoice and give a reason of at least three characters: who archived it, when and why stay on the invoice. Before you confirm, the dialog lists what archiving will do to this invoice: a confirmed bank match is unlinked and the transaction returns to its own review, pending match suggestions are discarded, a line already on a payment file is withdrawn, the invoice leaves its settlement session, an open request to the client is closed, and the dialog warns when the invoice has already been pushed to Business Central or has an intercompany twin, which you then settle by hand.

If the reason is that the invoice belongs to none of your companies, the company choice on the invoice offers the shortcut **Archive: no company of ours**. It opens the same dialog with the reason filled in and clears the company.

An archived invoice carries a grey **Archived** badge, and its banner shows who archived it, when and why, with a **Restore** button. Until it is restored it stays outside every list, count, review, approval queue and export. Restoring brings back the invoice and nothing else: a bank match that was unlinked is not re-created; if it was right, match it again by hand.

Archiving an invoice whose bookkeeping approval has already been given also needs the permission to approve bookkeeping; the dialog says so, and the button stays disabled without it.

## Downloading an invoice

Click **Download** on the invoice page to get the original file, or the **▾** arrow to choose a format:

| Format | Contents |
|--------|----------|
| **PDF** | The original document |
| **XLSX** | Two sheets: "Invoice" with the summary and "Line Items" with every line |
| **CSV** | The invoice header on the first rows, then the line items |
| **JSON** | All invoice fields and a `line_items` list |

The files are named after the original file. XLSX, CSV and JSON are built from the current data, so they include your corrections; their column headers are in English. An invoice without a document file (for example from NAV) has no Download button.

## Extraction and review

The upload panel shows each file as **Queued**, **Uploading…**, **Extracting…**, **Extracted** or **Failed**, with **Retry** for a failed one. While an invoice is still being read, its page shows the extraction state in the top card.

The **Review** label means the extraction scored below 80% for completeness: the invoice number, a date, a tax number, an amount or the line items are missing, or the totals do not add up. The invoice page shows the score as **Completeness: N%**. A failed extraction is labelled **Review** too. Check such invoices against the document and correct them in the editor.

## Who can do what

Seeing the workspace's invoices needs the permission to view invoices and bank transactions. A member without it still sees the invoices they uploaded themselves, and any invoice routed to them for approval: four tabs instead of six, no labels, no payment status and no to-dos, with a note on the list saying only their own invoices are shown.

Editing, archiving and restoring, marking or unmarking a duplicate, and the bulk actions all need the permission to edit invoices, which accountants hold by default (archiving an invoice whose bookkeeping approval is already given also needs the permission to approve bookkeeping); client users can view invoices, open the document and upload. See [Roles and Permissions](/roles-and-permissions).

## Tips

- **Suppliers and customers are partners.** If a supplier is not recognised, add or verify it under Master data, Partners. **Companies** are your own businesses. See [Managing Partners](/partners).
- **Use the History tab** to see who changed what and when.
- **Download as XLSX** to share an invoice with a colleague who works in Excel: the summary and the lines stay on separate sheets.
