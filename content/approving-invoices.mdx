# Approving Invoices for Payment

If your company uses payment approvals, the supplier invoices that need your yes or no wait for you under **Approvals** in the sidebar. This article is for the person who decides: the owner, the CEO, the department head.

## What you see

The **To approve** tab opens on the invoices due in the next 14 days that are assigned to you, earliest due date first. You do not need to filter anything. The number next to **Approvals** in the sidebar is how many invoices are assigned to you.

<ApprovalQueueFigure>
The **To approve** tab with four invoices waiting. The numbers match the list below. The companies and amounts are examples.
</ApprovalQueueFigure>

1. **Views**: switch between **Assigned to me**, **Unassigned** (invoices no rule could place, visible to every approver), **All awaiting** and **Due this week**.
2. **What you are approving**: the AI's one-line summary of the invoice. Click it to open the invoice itself.
3. **Supplier and invoice ID**: who is asking to be paid, and the invoice's ID in AI Finance Team.
4. **Due**: the payment date and how many days are left, or how many days it is overdue. An amber stripe on the left marks invoices due within 7 days or already overdue.
5. **Why you**: the reason the invoice landed with you. It is a rule your finance admin set up, a pattern learned from where earlier invoices of the same supplier went, the AI's pick, or the fallback approver when nothing else applies.
6. **Amount**: the gross amount in the invoice's currency.

## Approving

Click **Approve** on a row. That is the whole decision. AI Finance Team records who approved, when, and exactly which amount to which payee, so a later change to the invoice cannot be mistaken for what you agreed to.

To approve several at once, tick the rows. A bar shows how many you picked and their total in each currency; click **Approve selected**. The confirmation lists every supplier and the totals per currency before anything happens. Approving in bulk is available only if your finance admin left it switched on.

## Declining

Click **Decline** when the invoice should not be paid as it stands. A note is required. Click one of the four quick reasons to start the note, then add your own words. The note goes onto the invoice, where the accountant and the finance admin see it.

<DeclineDialogFigure>
Declining the roof repair until the work is finished. Clicking a quick reason writes it into the note, and **Decline** stays disabled until the note has text.
</DeclineDialogFigure>

Declining does not delete or reject the invoice in the books. It only says the payment is not cleared. The finance admin can reopen it once the question is settled with the supplier.

## Not mine

If an invoice reached you by mistake, click **Not mine**. It goes back to the finance admin's unrouted tray, and the routing learns from it.

## What happens after your decision

<ApprovalLifecycleFigure>
Each button leads somewhere different. Only an approved invoice moves on to payment; the other two come back for a new decision.
</ApprovalLifecycleFigure>

- **Approved** and **Declined** invoices show the matching pill in the invoice list and on the invoice itself.
- If the amount or the supplier on an approved invoice changes afterwards, the approval is reset and the invoice comes back to you. The invoice's history says why.
- An invoice that gets paid, cancelled or rejected by the accountant while it is waiting leaves your queue on its own.

## Invoices you do not see

Invoices the extraction flagged as doubtful are held by the accountant until the numbers are checked. They are not shown to approvers, so you are never asked to approve a number nobody has verified.

## Who can approve

Approving needs the permission "Approve invoices for payment" plus the right to view invoices. By default the client owner and the accountant admin have it. Your workspace admin can give it to anyone through a role or a per-member exception. See [Roles and Permissions](/roles-and-permissions).

## Emails

Four emails keep you informed without opening the app. Each has its own switch under **Account**, **Notifications**, and a one-click unsubscribe link in the footer.

<ApprovalEmailsFigure>
The **Payment approvals** section under **Account**, **Notifications**, with all four switched on. Each row says when that email arrives.
</ApprovalEmailsFigure>

The emails link into the app. There is no approve button in an email: a decision always happens signed in, on the invoice.
