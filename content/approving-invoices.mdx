# Approving Invoices for Payment

If your company uses payment approvals, the supplier invoices that need your yes or no wait for you under **Approvals** in the sidebar. This article is for the person who decides: the owner, the CEO, the department head.

## What you see

The **To approve** tab opens on the invoices due in the next 14 days that are assigned to you. You do not need to filter anything. Each row shows:

- **Due**: the payment date and how many days are left, or how many days it is overdue.
- **What you are approving**: the AI's one-line summary of the invoice as the headline, with the supplier and the invoice number under it. Click the number to open the invoice itself.
- **Why you**: the reason the invoice landed with you, for example "Rule: invoices from Bau-Trans Kft. go to you" or "Fallback approver".
- **Amount**: the gross amount in the invoice's currency.

The chips above the list switch between **Assigned to me**, **Unassigned** (invoices no rule could place, visible to every approver), **All awaiting** and **Due this week**. The count on the sidebar item is the number of invoices assigned to you.

## Approving

Click **Approve** on a row. That is the whole decision. AI Finance Team records who approved, when, and exactly which amount to which payee, so a later change to the invoice cannot be mistaken for what you agreed to.

To approve several at once, tick the rows and click **Approve selected**. The confirmation lists every supplier and the totals per currency before anything happens. Approving in bulk is available only if your finance admin left it switched on.

## Declining

Click **Decline** when the invoice should not be paid as it stands. A note is required. Four quick reasons are offered (wrong amount, work not done yet, already paid, not my decision), and you can add your own words. The note goes onto the invoice, where the accountant and the finance admin see it.

Declining does not delete or reject the invoice in the books. It only says the payment is not cleared. The finance admin can reopen it once the question is settled with the supplier.

## Not mine

If an invoice reached you by mistake, click **Not mine**. It goes back to the finance admin's unrouted tray, and the routing learns from it.

## What happens after your decision

- **Approved** invoices show an "Approved" pill in the invoice list and on the invoice. Once the bank payment arrives and is matched, the invoice is paid like any other.
- **Declined** invoices show a "Declined" pill and wait for the finance admin.
- If the amount or the supplier on an approved invoice changes afterwards, the approval is reset and the invoice comes back to you. The invoice's history says why.
- An invoice that gets paid, cancelled or rejected by the accountant while it is waiting leaves your queue on its own.

## Invoices you do not see

Invoices the extraction flagged as doubtful are held by the accountant until the numbers are checked. They are not shown to approvers, so you are never asked to approve a number nobody has verified.

## Who can approve

Approving needs the permission "Approve invoices for payment" plus the right to view invoices. By default the client owner and the accountant admin have it. Your workspace admin can give it to anyone through a role or a per-member exception. See [Roles and Permissions](/roles-and-permissions).
