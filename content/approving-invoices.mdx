# Approving Invoices for Payment

If your company uses payment approvals, the supplier invoices that need your yes or no wait for you under **Approvals** in the sidebar. This article is for the person who decides: the owner, the CEO, the department head.

## What you see

**Approvals** opens on your desk: one tile for each list you decide in, showing how many you can decide now, and a short list of what is waiting for you. The **Payment** tile, or the **Payment** tab, opens the payment list. It starts on the invoices due in the next 14 days that are assigned to you, earliest due date first, each on its own card. You do not need to filter anything. The number next to **Approvals** in the sidebar is how many invoices are assigned to you.

</ApprovalQueueFigure>

1. **Views**: switch between **Assigned to me**, **Unassigned** (invoices no rule could place, visible to every approver), **All awaiting** and **Due this week**.
2. **What you are approving**: the AI's one-line summary of the invoice. Click it to open the invoice in a preview over the list (see [below](#looking-at-an-invoice-without-leaving-the-list)).
3. **Supplier and invoice ID**: who is asking to be paid, and the invoice's ID in AI Finance Team.
4. **Due**: the payment date and how many days are left, or how many days it is overdue. An overdue date is red, and a date within the next 7 days is amber.
5. **Why you**: at the foot of the card, who holds the invoice and the reason it landed with you. It is a rule your finance admin set up, a pattern learned from where earlier invoices of the same supplier went, the AI's pick, or the fallback approver when nothing else applies.
6. **Amount**: the gross amount in the invoice's currency.
7. **Bank account**: where the money will go. A confirmed account shows its number. An amber label means someone has to look before paying: **First time seen** (nobody has confirmed an account for this supplier yet), **Account changed** (the invoice names a different account than the confirmed one) or **Invalid account**. When the account changed, check it with the supplier on a phone number you already know, not one printed on the invoice.

## Approving

Click **Approve** on a card. That is the whole decision. A green **Approved** stamp lands on the card and it leaves the list. AI Finance Team records who approved, when, and exactly which amount to which payee, so a later change to the invoice cannot be mistaken for what you agreed to.

To approve several at once, tick the cards. A bar shows how many you picked and their total in each currency; click **Approve selected**. The confirmation lists every supplier and the totals per currency before anything happens. Approving in bulk is available only if your finance admin left it switched on.

## Declining

Click **Decline** on the card when the invoice should not be paid as it stands. The card asks why, and a reason is required. Click one of the four quick reasons to start it, add your own words, then click **Decline** again. A red **Declined** stamp lands on the card and it leaves the list. The reason goes onto the invoice, where the accountant and the finance admin see it, and into **Decisions**.

<DeclineDialogFigure>
Declining the roof repair until the work is finished. Clicking a quick reason writes it into the reason, and **Decline** stays disabled until the reason has text.
</DeclineDialogFigure>

Declining does not delete or reject the invoice in the books. It only says the payment is not cleared. The finance admin can reopen it once the question is settled with the supplier.

## Not mine

If an invoice reached you by mistake, click **Not mine**. It goes back to the finance admin's unrouted tray, and the routing learns from it.

## Looking at an invoice without leaving the list

Click the summary on a card and the invoice opens in a preview over the list. Close the preview and you are back at the same card, with the list as you left it.

The preview shows what a decision needs: the amounts and dates, the supplier and your company, the bank account, the invoice lines, the matched payments and where each approval stands. **Document** shows the original invoice. The history, the messages and the booking details stay on the full invoice page.

- The arrows at the top, next to the position (for example `3 / 12`), move to the previous or next card. The ↑ and ↓ keys do the same.
- **Approve**, **Decline** and **Not mine** at the bottom work exactly like the buttons on the card, including the reason for a decline. After a decision the preview moves on to another waiting card, and closes when none is left.
- **Full invoice** opens the complete invoice page in a new browser tab.
- **Esc**, the **×** or a click outside the preview closes it.

To skip the preview, hold **Cmd** (**Ctrl** on Windows) while you click, or click with the middle mouse button. The full invoice page opens in a new tab.

The invoice links on your desk and on **Decisions** open the same preview, without the decision buttons.

## What happens after your decision

<ApprovalLifecycleFigure>
Each button leads somewhere different. Only an approved invoice moves on to payment; the other two come back for a new decision.
</ApprovalLifecycleFigure>

- **Approved** and **Declined** invoices show the matching pill in the invoice list and on the invoice itself.
- If the amount or the supplier on an approved invoice changes afterwards, the approval is reset and the invoice comes back to you. The invoice's history says why.
- An invoice that gets paid, cancelled or rejected by the accountant while it is waiting leaves your queue on its own.

## Invoices you do not see

Invoices the extraction flagged as doubtful are held by the accountant until the numbers are checked. They are not shown to approvers, so you are never asked to approve a number nobody has verified.

## Who can approve

Approving needs the permission "Approve invoices for payment" plus the right to view invoices. By default the finance lead and the accountant admin have it. Your workspace admin can give it to anyone through a role or a per-member exception. See [Roles and Permissions](/roles-and-permissions).

## Emails

Four emails keep you informed without opening the app. Each has its own switch under **Account**, **Notifications**, and a one-click unsubscribe link in the footer.

<ApprovalEmailsFigure>
The **Payment approvals** section under **Account**, **Notifications**, with all four switched on. Each row says when that email arrives.
</ApprovalEmailsFigure>

The emails link into the app. There is no approve button in an email: a decision always happens signed in, on the invoice.
