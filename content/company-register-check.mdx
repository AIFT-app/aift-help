# Checking Suppliers Against the Company Register

AI Finance Team can check your suppliers against the Hungarian company register. It tells you whether the bank account on a supplier invoice is the one the supplier has reported to the register, fills in partner details you are missing, and lists anything that differs so you can look at it. This article is for whoever approves or pays supplier invoices, and for whoever looks after partner master data.

The check is switched on per workspace. If you don't see it, ask your AI Finance Team contact.

## Where you see it

- **On a supplier invoice**, on the payment approval card, under the payee account. One line tells you what the register says about that account.
- **On a verified partner**, on the **Partner** tab, in the **Company register** section: what the register says about the company, what it filled in for you, and the differences to look at.

## What the three answers mean

- **Registered in the company register for this tax number.** The register lists this bank account for the supplier's tax number. The line also says since when, and when it was checked.
- **Not found in the company register for this tax number.** The register does not list this account for the supplier. **This is not proof of fraud.** A bank has eight days to report a newly opened account, so a recent account may still be genuine. Check with the supplier on a phone number you already know before paying.
- **Cannot be checked.** The register could not answer, and the reason is shown: the partner has no tax number, the supplier is not Hungarian, the supplier is a sole trader, the register has no company with this tax number, there is no bank account to compare, or the register could not be reached. Cannot be checked is never a negative result.

On a partner with no bank account on file, the **Company register** section says **Found in the register** instead: the lookup worked, there was just no account to compare.

Nothing here blocks an approval or a payment file. The check adds evidence to your decision; it does not make the decision.

## Accounts confirmed automatically

Before an account can go into a payment file, someone has to confirm it (see [Paying Approved Invoices](/paying-approved-invoices)). The register can do that confirmation for you in one situation only: the **first** account ever seen for a supplier, when all of these hold:

- the register lists exactly that account for the supplier's tax number,
- the company is active in the register,
- the partner's name agrees with the register,
- the tax number was not found by a name search (see below).

The approval card and the partner page then say the account was **confirmed by the company register**, with the date, so you can always tell it apart from an account a person confirmed.

**A changed account is never confirmed automatically.** When an invoice carries a different account from the one already confirmed, a person has to confirm the new one, whatever the register says. That is the invoice fraud control, and the register does not replace it. A changed account is always checked against the register afresh.

## What the register fills in, and what it never changes

When the register knows something you don't, such as the partner's address, it fills in the empty field. It **never overwrites** a value you already hold. If your value differs from the register, the section lists the difference instead:

- the company is no longer active (for example under liquidation),
- the name or the address differs,
- the tax number differs,
- you hold an account the register does not list for this company,
- the register lists accounts you don't hold (shown as one line with a count, and marked in the register's list).

Each difference is for you to look at. Nothing is changed until you change it.

## Checks go out of date

A check is valid for 90 days. After that the section marks it **Out of date**. Nothing re-checks itself, because every check uses AI Finance Team's monthly register lookups. Press **Revalidate** when you want a fresh answer. You need the **Edit master data** permission for this (see [Roles & Permissions](/roles-and-permissions)).

New supplier invoices start a check on their own. Invoices that arrived before the check was switched on do not.

## Partners without a tax number

A partner with no tax number cannot be checked. Press **Find in the company register** to search the register by the partner's name. If it finds the company, press **Use this tax number** on the right result.

A tax number found by name is marked as such. It fills the gap, but it is **never** used to confirm a bank account automatically, because a name is not proof of identity. If you later type the tax number in yourself, it counts as yours.

## Sole traders and private persons

Sole traders are listed in the register as private persons, so they are not checked, and AI Finance Team keeps nothing about them from the register except that they are sole traders. The partner used for private-person sales is never sent to the register at all.

## Where the data goes

The lookups are made at WellData Kft. (cegadatapi.hu), a Hungarian provider in the EU. What is sent is the supplier's tax number, or its name for a name search. WellData is listed in our sub-processor register.
