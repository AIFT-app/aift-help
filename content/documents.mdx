# Documents

**Documents** is where contracts and other paperwork live: everything that matters to the books but is not an invoice or a bank statement. A lease, a loan agreement, a costing manual, a chart of accounts, a grant decision.

You will find it in the workspace sidebar as **Documents**. It is an accountant's page; clients do not see it.

## Getting Documents In

Two ways, and the first needs no effort:

- **Forward them by email.** Anything sent to the workspace email address that is not an invoice or a statement lands here on its own. See [Forwarding Documents by Email](/email-forwarding).
- **Upload them.** Click **Upload**, then drag files in or browse. PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 20 MB each.

## What Happens on Arrival

Every document is read once, briefly, so the list is useful without opening anything:

- It is classified as **Contract** or **Other**.
- It gets a one or two sentence **summary**, shown on the card with how confident the AI is.
- Its language is detected.
- If the same file is already in the workspace, the copy is marked **Duplicate** and set aside rather than processed again.

This first pass is deliberately shallow. It reads the opening of the document to route and describe it, not to extract its contents.

## Finding Things

The filters above the list are **All**, **Contracts**, **Other**, **Needs review**, and **Duplicates**. Each document also carries a status:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Ready** | Classified, with a summary |
| **Review** | The AI was not confident enough; check the type yourself |
| **Processing** | Still being read |
| **Failed** | The file could not be read, for example an encrypted PDF |

## Working with One Document

Open a document to see its summary, type, language, file and source, with a preview beside them. From here you can:

- **Download** or open the original file.
- **Re-classify** it. If something was routed here that is really an invoice, a bank statement or a receipt, send it where it belongs and it is processed properly.
- **Mark irrelevant** to archive it, for anything that does not need to be kept in view.
- **Not a duplicate**, on a document set aside as a copy, to restore it for review.

## Building a Category List from a Document

If a document describes how the business divides its costs, you can turn it straight into a custom category list instead of typing it in. Click **Create category list**, and the AI reads up to twenty pages and proposes the list: a title, the guidance text, and the categories with codes and descriptions.

Nothing is created until you say so. Every field is editable, categories can be excluded, and the last step shows exactly what will be created before you commit. A costing manual, a chart of accounts, a department list and a grant budget are all good candidates; most other documents contain nothing of the sort, and the screen says so plainly rather than inventing one.

Once the list exists, the AI applies it to invoices and bank transactions, and reports can be broken down by it. See [Custom Categories](/custom-categories) for the whole picture, and [Reports](/reports) for reading a broken-down report.

A document shows which lists were built from it under **Produced from this**, so the origin of a set of codes stays traceable long after the fact.
