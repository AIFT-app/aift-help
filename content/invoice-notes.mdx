# Invoice Notes

You can write notes on an invoice on its **Notes** tab. For each note you choose who can see it: only you, your own side (the accounting firm or the client), or everyone who can see the invoice. Nobody receives an email about a note.

## Where to find them

Open the invoice and click the **Notes** tab, between **Attachments** and **Messages**. When the invoice has notes you can see, their number stands next to the tab name. With none, there is no number.

## Who can see a note

When you write a note, choose in **Who can see it?**:

| Choice | Who sees the note |
|--------|-------------------|
| **A note for myself** | Only you. |
| **Internal note** | Your side: the accountant side if you work at the accounting firm, the client side if you work at the client. The other side does not see it. |
| **Visible to everyone** | Everyone who can see the invoice, on both sides. |

A new note starts as **Internal note**. The line under the field always says what your choice means.

"Your side" is the side the writer belonged to when the note was written.

Everyone who can open the invoice can write notes, on both sides. A note does not change the invoice, so no extra permission is needed.

## Writing a note

1. Open the invoice's **Notes** tab.
2. Type the text, at most 2000 characters.
3. Choose who can see it.
4. Click **Add note** (or press Ctrl+Enter, Cmd+Enter on a Mac).

The note appears at the top of the list, with your name, your role, who can see it, and the date and time.

## Editing and deleting

Only the writer can change a note. On your own note, open the **⋯** menu:

- **Edit** opens the text and the **Who can see it?** field. Click **Save**. When the text changes, the note shows **edited**; a change of who can see it does not.
- **Delete** asks once. A deleted note cannot be restored.

## What notes do not do

- They send no email and no notification. When you need an answer, write on the **Messages** tab instead.
- They do not appear on the **History** tab.
- They never appear in exports and are never sent to Business Central.
- They do not change the invoice's data, categories, VAT codes or matching.

You can write notes on archived invoices too. When an uploaded invoice is later merged with the same invoice from NAV, the notes written on the upload appear on the NAV invoice.
