# Roles & Permissions

This article describes who can do what in AI Finance Team: how an accounting firm and its clients are organised, which permissions exist and who holds them, how custom roles and exceptions change them, and how people join, are invited and are removed.

## How the platform is organised

AI Finance Team connects accounting firms with their clients:

- An **organization** is an accounting firm or practice.
- A **workspace** is one client engagement: the firm works in it, and the client's people use it too. Accountants usually work across many workspaces; client users see only their own.

Every workspace member is on the **firm side** (they work for the accounting firm) or the **client side** (they work for the client). The side comes from how the person joined. It belongs to the membership, not to a role, and no role can change it. Some permissions are firm-only: they can never be given to someone on the client side.

## The organization

Members of an accounting firm have an organization role, **Admin** or **Member**. When no workspace is open, the sidebar shows **Organization** with these pages:

- **General**, **Manage workspaces**, **Team**, **AI access** and **Support access**, for every member of the firm;
- **Billing**, for admins, where billing is switched on.

Admins invite people to the firm, make a member an admin or an admin a member, and remove people. Removing someone from the firm also removes them from every workspace of the firm; the last admin cannot be removed. Members see the firm's team but cannot change it.

Client users have no organization role. They exist only in the workspaces they were invited to.

## Permissions

Every workspace member has one **role**, and a role is a named set of **permissions**. Every workspace has five **system roles**, and its managers can add **custom roles** for that workspace.

The ticks are the **defaults of the five system roles**. A custom role picks its own set, and an exception changes a single member. "Push into the accounting system" also needs the connection to be switched on for the workspace.

Two things are fixed by rule and are never part of a role:

- **Connecting and disconnecting bank accounts.** Any client-side member can connect a bank; disconnecting a bank connection belongs to the finance lead. Bank credentials always stay on the client side.
- **NAV Online Számla credentials** can be set up by the finance lead, an accountant admin or an accountant. Nobody can read them back.

Both are decided by the member's **base role** (the system role a custom role is based on), so a custom role based on Finance lead also owns the bank connection.

What the broader permissions cover:

- **Edit master data**: partners, categories, transaction types, the VAT setup, custom category lists and the ledger defaults.
- **Edit workspace settings**: numbering, currency, AI and data settings.
- **Manage connections**: the workspace's email address for incoming documents and the Business Central link.
- **Approve invoices for payment**, **Manage approvals** and **Export payment file**: see [Setting Up Payment Approvals](/setting-up-approvals) and [Paying Approved Invoices](/paying-approved-invoices).

### System roles

| Role | Side | Typical user |
|---|---|---|
| Accountant admin | Firm | Lead accountant on the engagement: everything, including the team and the settings |
| Accountant | Firm | Supporting accountant: all bookkeeping work, no team or settings |
| Finance lead | Client | Senior person at the client: uploads, invites and manages the client's people and connections, approves invoices, owns the bank connection |
| Finance admin | Client | On the finance lead's team: runs the approval process and produces the payment file, but does not decide an invoice |
| Team member | Client | Other people at the client: uploads and views |

System roles cannot be changed. Click **View** on one to see, read-only, what a member on that role can do.

**Finance admin** is new, and nobody holds it until a manager assigns it. It exists because preparing a payment run and deciding it are different jobs: a Finance admin can run the approval process and export the payment file, while approving an invoice stays with the finance lead.

### Custom roles

Open **Settings → Team management → Roles** in the workspace.

<RolesTabFigure>

</RolesTabFigure>

1. The tabs of Team management.
2. A system role, with **View** and **Duplicate**.
3. A custom role, based on a system role of its side.
4. **Add role**.

- **Add role** creates a role with nothing ticked; **Duplicate** starts from an existing role's permissions. A custom role is based on a system role of the same side (on the client side: Team member, Finance admin or Finance lead), and a firm-only permission is not offered for a client-side role.
- You can only give permissions you hold yourself.
- Everyone who opens the tab sees every role, but a client-side manager cannot change firm-side roles.
- Assign a role on a member's access page, or pick it when inviting someone.
- A role that members still hold cannot be archived; move them to another role first.
- Custom roles belong to the workspace they were created in.

Parts of the app that have not moved to permissions yet still behave like the member's base role, as the Roles tab notes.

### Exceptions for one member

On the **Team members** tab, **Edit access** next to a member opens their access page. There a manager can change the member's role and add an exception: give one permission the role lacks, or take away one it has. The rules:

- you can only give or take away a permission you hold yourself;
- you cannot change your own access;
- an exception that the role already answers the same way is refused.

The page lists **What this member can do**, and where each answer comes from: **From role**, **Exception, added**, **Exception, removed**, **Fixed by rule** or **Not available on the client side**. Every member can see their own access under **My access**.

Taking away "View invoices and bank transactions" makes a member an upload-only user: they open the workspace, see the member list and their own access, and can upload documents, but every record list is empty and each page says so.

### Managing the team

"Manage members and roles" is what makes someone a manager of the team. It lets them change a member's role, set exceptions, remove members and, on the firm side, decide join requests. A client-side manager (the finance lead by default) manages client-side members only. The last accountant admin and the last finance lead of a workspace cannot be removed, cannot leave, and cannot be moved to another base role.

### What a member sees

The sidebar follows the member's side and permissions, but hiding a menu item is not the protection: what a member can read or change is decided by their permissions in the database. A custom role without "Close periods" is turned away from Closing however they got there.

## How membership works

### Inviting people

The workspace's **Settings → Team management → Invite a new member** (for members holding "Invite members") and the organization's **Team** page (for organization admins) have the same invite panel. It queues up to **10 invitations** at once:

- One row per person: the email address, the role, and optionally their **Name** and **Language**. With a name, the invitation email greets them by name and the sign-up form is filled in.
- An optional message, up to 500 characters, shared by the whole batch. Links in it become clickable.
- Click **Send invite**, or **Send N invites** for several. Rows that were sent drop out of the form; rows that failed (for example a duplicate email address) stay with the error so you can fix them.

The role list depends on who invites: a client-side inviter can invite a Team member or a Finance admin, never another Finance lead; a firm-side inviter can invite any workspace role; and the organization panel invites to the firm.

AI Finance Team emails each person the invitation link and your message; the link is also shown to you in case the email does not arrive. Each invitation expires after 14 days, can be revoked before it is accepted, and only works for the email address it was sent to.

### Accepting an invitation

When the person opens the link:

- if they are not signed in, they sign in or sign up, and the invitation is kept through it;
- if they are signed in with a different email address, **Wrong account?** offers **Sign in with a different account**;
- once accepted, a **workspace invitation** adds them to the workspace and opens it; an invitation with an accountant role also makes them a member of the firm. An **organization invitation** adds them to the firm's team.

### Joining a workspace

There are three ways into a workspace:

1. **An invitation**, as above.
2. **An organization admin adds themselves**: under **Organization → Manage workspaces**, they pick a role (Accountant by default) and click **Add me**. An admin who opens a firm workspace they are not a member of sees a banner with **Add me as a member**.
3. **An organization member asks to join**: under **Organization → Manage workspaces**, they pick Accountant or Accountant admin and click **Ask to join**. The workspace's accountant admins and the firm's admins get an email. The request is decided in the workspace under **Settings → Team management → Pending requests** by a firm-side member holding "Manage members and roles": **Approve**, with the role requested or another one, or **Deny**. The person is told by email either way.

### Removing a member

A member holding "Manage members and roles" can remove a member on their own side from the **Team members** tab: by default, the accountant admin removes firm-side members and the finance lead removes client-side members. Any member can leave a workspace themselves, except the last accountant admin and the last finance lead. Removed people keep their account; they only lose access to that workspace. Removing someone from the organization (on its **Team** page) removes them from every workspace of the firm.
