# Bank Transactions

The **Bank transactions** page lists every movement on your companies' bank accounts. AI Finance Team suggests a category for each transaction and matches it to its invoice, and the list shows where each one stands.

Transactions arrive in two ways:

- **From a connected bank account**, twice a day. See [Connecting Bank Accounts](/bank-accounts).
- **From an uploaded bank statement** (PDF), with **Upload statement** at the top right of the page. See [Uploading Bank Statements](/bank-statement-upload).

When a statement line is the same movement the bank connection already brought in, the two become one transaction with both sources.

1. **Date**: the booking date, with AI Finance Team's own ID underneath when your workspace shows IDs. Click the ID to copy it.
2. **Partner**, with the bank's description underneath on up to two lines. A click anywhere on the row opens the transaction.
3. **Labels**: what the transaction needs comes first (**Unmatched**, **Duplicate**), then **Matched** or **No invoice**, and where it came from (**Bank feed**, **Statement**) last.
4. **Category**: the category's ledger code first, then its name.
5. **Amount**: in the account's currency, green with an up arrow for money in, red with a down arrow for money out.

Click **Bank transactions** in the sidebar. The list has these columns: **Date** with the **ID** under it (when your workspace shows it), **Partner**, **Labels**, **Company**, **Category** and **Amount**. **Company** appears only when your workspace has more than one company. It is the holder of the bank account, or, on an account several companies share (a [cash pool](/cash-pool)), the company of the matched invoice.

The list shows 50 transactions per page, newest first, and can be sorted by date, ID, partner or amount. An amber left edge marks a transaction that needs action. The list uses the full width of the window. In a narrow window it scrolls sideways, and the **Amount** column stays in view on the right.

The **Category** cell is empty when **Use cash-flow categories for bookkeeping only** is on in **Settings → Ledger defaults** and the transaction still expects an invoice. With that setting the ledger account of such a transaction comes from its invoice, not from the category. See [Categorising Invoices & Transactions](/categorization).

### Labels

| Label | Colour | Meaning |
|-------|--------|---------|
| **Unmatched** | amber | No invoice matched yet, and not marked as needing none |
| **Duplicate** | amber | Flagged as a copy of another transaction on the same account, with the same booking date and amount. It stays on the list, and matching leaves it out |
| **Matched** | green | Matched to one or more invoices, automatically or by hand |
| **No invoice** | blue | Marked as needing no invoice, by a transaction type or by a person |
| **Bank feed** | grey | Came from a connected bank account |
| **Statement** | grey | Came from an uploaded bank statement |

How a transaction gets matched is in [Invoice Matching](/invoice-matching). Bank fees, taxes and transfers between your own accounts need no invoice: see [Transaction Types & No Invoice Needed](/transaction-types).

### Finding transactions

Search by partner, description, reference or AI Finance Team ID. The partner search covers both the name the bank sent and the partner it is linked to.

Above the list you can filter by date range, **Company** (when you have more than one), **Account** and **Direction** (**Money in** or **Money out**). When an account has no company yet, the **Company** filter also offers **No company assigned**.

The pills filter by label: **Bank feed**, **Statement**, **Duplicate**, **Unmatched**, **Matched** and **No invoice**. Several can be on at once:

- Within **Bank feed**, **Statement** and **Duplicate**, a transaction needs any one of them.
- Within **Unmatched**, **Matched** and **No invoice**, the same.
- Across the two groups it needs both. **Statement** with **Unmatched** lists the unmatched transactions that came from a statement.

**Custom category missing** lists the transactions where a mandatory [custom category list](/custom-categories) is still empty. If you work in a [focused period](/focused-period), the list follows it.

With the permission "Edit bank transactions" you can tick rows and **Re-categorize** them. Transactions the AI categorised, or that have no category, are categorised again against your current categories. Categories set by hand stay as they are.

## Opening a transaction

Click a row to open the transaction. On its page, **j** or the down arrow goes to the next transaction of the list, **k** or the up arrow to the previous one, with your filters and sort order kept. The **Matching & payment** card shows the matched invoices and the actions for matching; see [Invoice Matching](/invoice-matching).

The **Parties** card shows both sides in the order the money moves: for a payment out, your company on top (**Payer**) and the partner below (**Payee**), the other way round for money in. Your company shows the full number of the bank account. Clicking either side's tile (it shows **Details**) opens a read-only side panel, the same one as on an invoice. If you may make changes, **Edit** shows the partner picker, and the company picker when several companies share the bank account. For a partner that is missing or not verified yet the card opens like that by itself, and the **Verify partner** button and the to-do of the same name lead there.

## Statements

The **Statements** tab next to the list shows every uploaded bank statement with its status. See [Uploading Bank Statements](/bank-statement-upload).

## Who sees what

- **"View invoices and bank transactions"** shows the whole list. Every role has it by default; see [Roles & Permissions](/roles-and-permissions).
- **Without it**, a member who uploaded bank statements sees only the transactions of those statements, with a note above the list. They see no filter pills, no categories and no matching labels: only **Bank feed** and **Statement**.
- **"Edit bank transactions"** is needed to re-categorise, to change a transaction and to mark it as needing no invoice. By default only the two accountant roles have it.

Until the workspace has a bank account, the page shows **No bank accounts yet**, with a prompt to connect an account or upload a statement.
