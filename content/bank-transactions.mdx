# Bank Transactions

The **Bank transactions** page lists every movement on your companies' bank accounts. AI Finance Team suggests a category for each transaction and matches it to its invoice, and the list shows where each one stands.

Transactions arrive in two ways:

- **From a connected bank account**, twice a day. See [Connecting Bank Accounts](/bank-accounts).
- **From an uploaded bank statement** (PDF), with **Upload statement** at the top right of the page. See [Uploading Bank Statements](/bank-statement-upload).

When a statement line is the same movement the bank connection already brought in, the two become one transaction with both sources.

4. **Category**: the category's ledger code first, then its name.
5. **Amount**: in the account's currency, green with an up arrow for money in, red with a down arrow for money out.

Click **Bank transactions** in the sidebar. The list has these columns: **Date** with the **ID** under it (when your workspace shows it), **Partner**, **Labels**, **Company**, **Category** and **Amount**. **Company** appears only when your workspace has more than one company. It is the holder of the bank account, or, on an account several companies share (a [cash pool](/cash-pool)), the company of the matched invoice.

The list shows 50 transactions per page, newest first, and can be sorted by date, ID, partner or amount. An amber left edge marks a transaction with a to-do. The list uses the full width of the window. In a narrow window it scrolls sideways, and the **Amount** column stays in view on the right.

The **Category** cell is empty when **Use cash-flow categories for bookkeeping only** is on in **Settings → Ledger defaults** and the transaction still expects an invoice. With that setting the ledger account of such a transaction comes from its invoice, not from the category. See [Categorising Invoices & Transactions](/categorization).

### To-dos and labels

The chips under the filter strip are the to-dos, each with its count; a chip with nothing to do is hidden. A chip lists its transactions, and with several chips on, a transaction needs any one of them. A selected chip is blue with a ✕. A row shows its to-dos in amber in the same words, and the transaction's **To do** card lists them with the way to clear each one.

**Collapse** folds the to-do row into one **To do** button in the filter strip, with a number for the to-dos you selected. Hover over it or click it to see the to-dos as a list with their counts; **Expand to-dos** at the bottom of the list brings the row back. Your browser remembers the choice for you on this list, separately from the Invoices list; the row is open by default.

| To-do | When | How it clears |
|-------|------|---------------|
| **Incomplete categorization** | The transaction has no category. The row says why: **suggestion waiting** (the AI's suggestion waits for you), **failed**, or nothing more when no suggestion was made. Only while transaction categorization is on and the workspace has transaction categories. Not shown on a transaction matched to an invoice, because the invoice carries the booking | Accept the suggestion or choose a category; categorize a failed one again |
| **Custom category missing** | A mandatory [custom category list](/custom-categories) is still empty | Fill in the list on the transaction |
| **Unmatched** | No invoice is matched to it, it is not an internal transfer, and it is not marked as needing none. These are the transactions the [Monthly Closing](/closing) counts as unresolved | Match it, mark it **No invoice**, or close it (see below) |
| **Partially matched** | Its invoices cover only part of its amount | Complete the settlement, add an invoice, or close it |
| **Match suggestion awaiting a decision** | A match suggestion waits for a decision | Confirm or reject the suggestion, or close the transaction |
| **Settlement in progress** | It is open in a settlement session | Commit or discard the session |
| **Unverified partner** | Its partner is not verified yet | Verify the partner, or choose another |
| **Exchange rate missing** | Its exchange rate could not be fetched | Enter the rate on the transaction (see below) |
| **GL contra account missing** | Only when the accounting software is Business Central: a transaction needing no invoice whose category has no ledger code | Give the category its code in master data, or choose another category |

The grey labels are information: **Matched**, **No invoice**, **Closed**, **Internal transfer**, **Duplicate**, **Bank feed** and **Statement**.

The to-dos belong to the accountant: a member who sees only their own statements sees none. [Mission Control](/mission-control) lists every workspace's transaction to-dos with the same counts as the chips.

How a transaction gets matched is in [Invoice Matching](/invoice-matching). Bank fees, taxes and transfers between your own accounts need no invoice: see [Transaction Types & No Invoice Needed](/transaction-types).

### Finding transactions

Search by partner, description, reference, AI Finance Team ID, amount or the partner's account number (IBAN, with or without spaces). The partner search covers both the name the bank sent and the partner it is linked to. Accents are optional, and the amount is read as a Hungarian number: “125 000” and “125000,00” are the same, for money in and money out. You can also search an amount range: “100 000 - 200 000” or “100000..200000” shows the lines between the two amounts. The matching text is highlighted in yellow. When the match is not visible in the row (the reference, the account number, or the ID when IDs are hidden), a **Match:** line under the partner says why the line is there.

In the strip above the list you filter by date range, by **Company** (when you have more than one; **No company assigned** when an account has no company yet) and by **Account** (when you have more than one bank account), and the **Direction** switch picks **All**, **Money in** or **Money out** in one click. With eight or more bank accounts you can type to search the **Account** list. The other filters are behind **More filters**, which opens them from the right; picking a value filters at once. A filter with more than nine values (**Category**, for example) shows them in their own scrolling box, with a search box above. The number on the button counts the filters set there, and every filter you set shows under the strip, where ✕ removes it. On a phone **Company**, **Account** and **Direction** move behind **More filters** too. A transaction must meet every filter you set, and any one of the values you pick within a filter. Behind **More filters**, in this order:

- **Matching**: **Unmatched**, **Partially matched**, **Unapplied partner payment**, **Matched automatically**, **Matched manually**, **No invoice**, **Closed**, **Internal transfer**, **Suggestion awaiting a decision** and **Settlement in progress**. An internal transfer is only ever **Internal transfer**. **Unapplied partner payment** shows exactly the records the Matching page's tile of the same name counts: payments linked to a verified partner that are not matched to an invoice, or only partly.
- **Coding**: **Suggestion awaiting acceptance**, **No category**, **Categorization failed**, **Custom category missing** and **Complete**.
- **Category**, **Transaction type** (or **No type**) and **Partner** (**Verified**, **Unverified** or **No partner**).
- **Source**: **Bank feed** or **Statement**.
- **Record state**: **Active** by default, or **Duplicate** or **All**. A duplicate is a copy of another transaction on the same account, with the same booking date and amount; matching leaves it out, and **Not a duplicate** on its page brings it back.


The Monthly Closing's links open the list filtered the way its check counts: unresolved transactions on the **Unmatched** chip, uncategorized ones on **Incomplete categorization**, and missing exchange rates under **Exchange rate pending or failed**. If you work in a [focused period](/focused-period), the list follows it.

With the permission "Edit bank transactions" you can tick rows and **Re-categorize** them. Transactions the AI categorised, or that have no category, are categorised again against your current categories. Categories set by hand stay as they are.

## Opening a transaction

Click a row to open the transaction. On its page, **j** or the down arrow goes to the next transaction of the list, **k** or the up arrow to the previous one, with your filters and sort order kept. The **Matching & payment** card shows the matched invoices and the actions for matching; see [Invoice Matching](/invoice-matching).

The **To do** card under the summary lists the transaction's to-dos, each leading to its fix. It also offers:

- **Close**, on an unmatched or partially matched transaction that needs no further matching, for example the remainder of a bank fee or one half of a refund pair, with an optional note. Its matching to-dos clear, its waiting match suggestions are dismissed and the invoice request to the client is closed; its category, partner and exchange-rate to-dos stay. A closed transaction counts as resolved in the Monthly Closing, keeps its matched invoices and is not sent to Business Central. **Reopen** brings the matching back, and marking it **No invoice** ends the closure too. Both need "Edit bank transactions" and "Manage matching".
- **Not a duplicate**, on a transaction marked as a duplicate: it returns to the active list, and its history shows it.
- A manual exchange rate when the rate could not be fetched: the rate to the base currency, the reporting rate, or both, whichever failed.

The **Parties** card shows both sides in the order the money moves: for a payment out, your company on top (**Payer**) and the partner below (**Payee**), the other way round for money in. Your company shows the full number of the bank account. Clicking either side's tile (it shows **Details**) opens a read-only side panel, the same one as on an invoice. If you may make changes, **Edit** shows the partner picker, and the company picker when several companies share the bank account. For a partner that is missing or not verified yet the card opens like that by itself, and the **Verify partner** button and the to-do of the same name lead there.

## Statements

The **Statements** tab next to the list shows every uploaded bank statement with its status. See [Uploading Bank Statements](/bank-statement-upload).

## Who sees what

- **"View invoices and bank transactions"** shows the whole list. Every role has it by default; see [Roles & Permissions](/roles-and-permissions).
- **Without it**, a member who uploaded bank statements sees only the transactions of those statements, with a note above the list. They see no to-dos, no categories and no matching labels: only **Bank feed** and **Statement**.
- **"Edit bank transactions"** is needed to re-categorise, to change a transaction and to mark it as needing no invoice. By default only the two accountant roles have it.

Until the workspace has a bank account, the page shows **No bank accounts yet**, with a prompt to connect an account or upload a statement.
