# Business Central

AI Finance Team connects to Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central: it pairs your companies with the ones in Business Central, copies their master data, and can create your partners there as vendor cards.

**Before anything else, we have to switch Business Central on for the workspace.** It is off by default, and while it is off the menu is not there at all. Ask us, and we will turn it on.

## What Is Read, and What Is Written

AI Finance Team **reads** the companies in your Business Central, and for each paired company its accounts, VAT and posting groups, units of measure, currencies, bank accounts, payment methods and terms, dimensions and dimension values, vendors and customers. **No balances are read.** Documents and ledger entries are read only to answer a specific question: what became of an invoice or a journal line we created (still a draft, posted, or gone); whether the invoice a bank payment pays is posted and still open in Business Central; and, in the troubleshooting checks, which customers and outgoing invoices exist, which purchase credit memos exist and where your credit notes stand, where a draft keeps its VAT date, and which fields Business Central exposes.

AI Finance Team **writes** three kinds of record, all behind a second switch that only we can turn on:

- a **vendor card**, created from one of your partners;
- a **purchase invoice**, created from one of your expense invoices;
- a **general journal line**, created from one of your bank lines.

Even then:

- **Nothing is ever posted.** The purchase invoice is created as a **draft**, the bank line as an **unposted journal line**, and both are left there. Posting is available over the interface and we deliberately do not use it: a document posted from wrong data has to be reversed, which marks the ledger for good, while a draft is deleted in one click. Posting stays the bookkeeper's act, inside Business Central.
- **An existing record is never rewritten.** A vendor card with the same tax number is linked to your partner instead, and an existing document is never edited. Sending an invoice again replaces a draft we created by deleting it, never a posted invoice (see Sending an invoice again).
- **We only ever delete what we created.** Six controls on the send screen delete: the probe removes the throwaway draft it just made, the bank journal probe removes its made-up lines and the AIFTSZONDA batch it created for them, the numbering trial and the two VAT date write probes each remove their made-up AIFT-TESZT draft (and any an earlier trial left behind), and the cleanup removes drafts AI Finance Team recorded sending but never managed to link. Sending an invoice again, from its own page, deletes the draft it replaces. Apart from these, the PDF upload deletes an empty attachment row it has just made itself (see The PDF), and the bank send deletes a journal line it has just written when Business Central recorded it differently from what we sent (see Sending Bank Lines). Nothing else is ever deleted, and nothing Business Central does not call a draft.

## Setting Up the Connection

Setting it up needs **two separate approvals**, usually given by two different people. Granting one does not grant the other. That is the single thing most worth understanding before you start.

1. **Microsoft approval.** Someone with **Global Administrator** rights in your Microsoft 365 approves AI Finance Team once. This is usually your IT administrator or IT provider. It takes about a minute, on a Microsoft page.
2. **Business Central registration.** Someone with **SUPER** rights inside Business Central adds our application there and gives it a permission set. This is often the accountant rather than the IT administrator.

The second one cannot be automated: Business Central has no interface for it, so a person does it by hand. Approval alone leaves the connection refusing every request, which is why AI Finance Team tells the two states apart.

Open **Settings → Business Central Connection** and click **Set up the connection**.

### Step 1: send the approval link

Choose **Create the approval link**. You get a link and a ready-written message to send to whoever administers your Microsoft 365.

- **Copy the link now.** It is stored hashed, so it cannot be shown again once you leave the page, and creating a new one cancels the old one. Each link lasts 7 days.
- If you are the Global Administrator yourself, use **I am the Microsoft admin, approve now**. That also mints a new link and cancels the current one.
- Microsoft will warn that **the publisher is not verified**. That is normal here and does not stop an administrator approving.
- The page the administrator sees does not name your company, because anyone holding the link can open it.

The permission granted is read and write on the Business Central API. It does not allow creating or deleting environments.

### Step 2: register the application in Business Central

The steps are on the page from the start, marked **After step 1** until Microsoft approval lands. **Copy these steps** puts the Application ID and all of them on your clipboard in one block, ready to send on.

1. In Business Central, press **Alt+Q** and search for **Microsoft Entra Applications**.
2. Choose **New** and paste in the Application ID.
3. Set the description to **AI Finance Team integration**.
4. Set **State** to **Enabled**.
5. Add the permission set **D365 BUS FULL ACCESS**.
6. Leave the **Company** field on that line **empty**.

Three things go wrong here more often than anything else:

- **Naming a company on that line** silently limits AI Finance Team to that one company. Everything appears to work and the other companies never show up. Leave it empty.
- **Using D365 AUTOMATION.** The connection succeeds and returns nothing useful, because that permission set covers administrative APIs rather than business data.
- **Using SUPER.** It works and grants far more than the integration needs.

The registration is **per environment**. Doing it in Production does not cover a Sandbox.

### What the statuses mean

| Status | What it means | Who acts next |
|---|---|---|
| Not connected | No approval link is waiting: none has been created yet, or the last one expired (links last 7 days) or was cancelled. | You. |
| Waiting for Microsoft approval | The link exists but nobody has approved it. | Your Microsoft administrator. |
| Waiting for Business Central registration | Our sign-in works; Business Central is still refusing, so step 2 is not done. | Your Business Central administrator. |
| Connected | We can read your companies. | Nobody. |
| Error | The page shows the code, and says whether this one is ours to fix. | Depends on the code. |

If the status says the environment could not be found, the page offers an **environment** field. It is **Production** for most customers; check the name in your own Business Central address. Saving it makes the connection unverified until the next successful check, so the Master data menu below disappears for a moment.

That menu, **Master data → Business Central**, appears only once the connection has worked at least once.

## Pairing Companies

Open **Master data → Business Central → Companies**. Every company in your Business Central is listed, grouped by what can be done with it: already paired, a suggested match, no match, already in AI Finance Team, and not a company.

Each Business Central company can be paired with one AI Finance Team company, and the other way round. There are two ways:

- **Create a company from a Business Central company.** The name, tax number, country and address are taken from Business Central. Tick several to create them in one go; creating one at a time shows you the values first and lets you correct the name.
- **Pair with a company you already have.**

A suggestion based on the **tax number** is reliable. A suggestion based on the **name** is only a suggestion.

You can also start from **Master data → Companies**, where **Create companies from Business Central** sits next to **Add company** and lists only what is not paired yet.

### One company per tax number

A workspace holds each company once. If a Business Central company's tax number already belongs to a company here, the row offers **Pair** only, never **Create company**, and it is left out of bulk selection. If that company is already paired with another Business Central company, a test copy of the same firm for example, the row says which one; unpair that first.

Where several Business Central companies share one tax number, the page says so and asks you to pick the right one, rather than guessing.

### Notices on a row

- **Different currency.** Business Central keeps that company's books in another currency than your base currency. A warning only.
- **Different country.**
- **Different tax numbers.** Check which is right before accepting.
- **The tax number is a VAT group identifier.** Your company states the group's number while Business Central states the member's own.
- **Details could not be read.**

A Business Central company whose tax number is a group registration rather than a company cannot become a company here. Leave it unpaired: it is a filing arrangement, not a business.

### What pairing writes

When you pair with an existing company you can copy the address and the tax number across. **Only empty fields are filled.** A value already in AI Finance Team is never overwritten, whatever you tick; edit the company itself to change it.

### Unpairing

**Unlink** on the row, or on the company's own page. It removes the pairing and **deletes the copied master data of that company** along with the links to any vendor and customer cards. The VAT mapping and the record of what was pushed are kept, and nothing changes in Business Central.

## Keeping the Copy Fresh

The copied master data refreshes itself: once a company's copy is more than 20 hours old, an hourly check reads it again, a few companies per run, oldest first. You do not need to ask for it.

## Where the Two Systems Differ

**Master data → Business Central → Differences** compares five kinds of record: **Accounts and invoice categories**, **VAT mapping**, **VAT product and service groups**, **Custom category lists** and **Partners**. Accounts and partners are compared in both directions; the other three exist only in AI Finance Team, so their Business Central side reads **not applicable**. Each cell offers what makes sense there, such as importing what is missing or opening the record.

Read the counts carefully. A count can read **not applicable** (there is nothing to compare on that side) or **not read** (the copy has not been read yet), and neither of those means zero. A genuine zero shows as 0.

## Accounts as Categories

Business Central cannot create a ledger account through its API, so the client's chart of accounts stays theirs. To let a category name a ledger account, bring the accounts in as categories.

Open **Master data → Accounting categories** and choose **Import**, then **Import accounts from Business Central**. The dropdown is on both the **Invoice** and the **Cash** tab.

**Create as** decides what each ticked account becomes: **Invoice categories**, **Cash categories**, or **Both**, which makes one of each with the same account number. They stay separate afterwards, so renaming one does not rename the other.

The review step is a spreadsheet-style grid, not a list of tick boxes. You can edit the name of a row, set its direction, filter by account kind, search by the start of an account number, and paste a column of numbers copied from Excel to tick them. Then choose **Create**: the next screen shows what was created and what was skipped, with a reason each.

**The account number identifies the category.** Two accounts with different numbers become two categories even with the same name, and an account number that already has a category of that kind is skipped.

### Copying invoice categories to cash categories

On the **Cash** tab, **Copy from invoice categories** creates a cash category for every invoice category the cash side lacks, with the same account number, name and direction. It shows the counts first and changes nothing that exists. Built-in categories are not copied.

## Vendors and Customers as Partners

**Import → Vendors from Business Central** on **Master data → Partners**, and **Import** on the Partners row of the Differences tab, brings vendor cards in as partners. They are matched on the tax number, and a card without a tax number on its name, so a vendor that already exists here is shown as such and cannot be added twice. Vendor cards without a tax number can still be added, but only the name can match them afterwards.

**Import → Customers from Business Central** does the same for customer cards. Business Central keeps a company you sell to as a customer card and a company you buy from as a vendor card, each with its own number. In AI Finance Team both are the same partner. So when a customer's tax number already belongs to a partner here, no new partner is created: its customer cards are **linked** to that partner, and the partner itself is not changed. The customer card of one of your own companies (a group company that invoices a sister company) is linked to that company's partner. Cards without a tax number are listed but never ticked in advance.

The partner's page lists, per company, its vendor card and its customer card, each with its number and its kind, and the same numbers appear under the partner's AI Finance Team code. A card blocked in Business Central is marked in both places: **Blocked** when it is blocked for everything (its number is greyed under the code), **Blocked for payments** when only payments are refused and invoices still post to it. Neither import offers a card that is blocked for everything. A card reaches a partner one of two ways: we find it by its tax number, or we also stored the link (at an import or a push), and then it is marked **Linked** as well. **Matched** means there is nothing to do about it: its name does not differ and it is not blocked. For sending invoices and for the Push list, a tax-number match is enough; such a card drops out if someone changes or removes the tax number on it in Business Central, or if the card is linked to another partner.

### Moving a Card to Another Partner

When a card sits on the wrong partner (say a merge left a group company's loan card on a bank's partner), the **Move** button under the card, in the **Business Central** section of the partner's page, takes it off. There are two ways:

- **To another partner**: find the partner the card belongs to by name or tax number.
- **As a new partner**: a new partner is created from the card, with the card's name (you can change it before saving), address and country. The tax number comes from the card when it is a real one that no other partner holds yet. **00000000** is not a tax number, so such a card makes a partner without one. The dialog says which case applies.

From then on the card belongs to its new partner. A link is stronger than the tax number: a card linked to another partner is no longer shown on the old partner by its tax number, and the old partner's invoices are not sent to it. Invoices, transactions and anything already sent to Business Central stay where they are; if an invoice sits on the wrong partner too, change it on the invoice. If the card was the old partner's default card, the default is cleared. The move appears on the **History** tab of both partners. Moving a card needs the "Edit master data" permission.

## VAT Mapping

**Master data → VAT codes → Business Central** maps each of your (product/service group, rate) pairs to one Business Central tax group code. It is saved a row at a time, and it survives unpairing.

A pair that has no Business Central tax group yet carries an amber **Not mapped** badge on its row. Those rows come first, and a line above the table says how many there are; under them the rows are ordered by how many invoice lines use them. **Until a pair is mapped, an invoice with such a line cannot be sent**: without a mapping Business Central would use the ledger account's own default group, which nobody chose (see Sending Invoices below).

## Partner VAT Groups from the Cards

Every vendor card and customer card in Business Central carries a VAT business posting group (for example BELFÖLD or EU SZÁLLÍTÓ). A partner that has a card takes its partner VAT group from it, so nobody has to set it by hand.

The **Partner VAT groups from the cards** table on **Master data → VAT codes → Business Central** lists the codes found on the cards, and how many vendor cards and customer cards carry each. For every code, choose the partner VAT group it means. A code we recognise by its name shows a proposal, and **Accept proposals** saves them all at once. Two codes can mean the same group: EU SZÁLLÍTÓ and EU VEVŐ are both the EU group.

Saving the mapping changes no partner yet. An amber box above the table shows how many partners would change VAT group, how many invoice lines would get a VAT code, and how many would change theirs. **Apply** writes them. If you do not press it, the next Business Central refresh does.

From then on Business Central is the master:

- **The VAT group of a partner that has a card cannot be changed in AI Finance Team.** It is read only on the **Rules** tab of the partner, with its cards and their codes under it. To change the group, change the card in Business Central. The next refresh of that company brings it over: once a day on its own, or at once with **Refresh all** on the Differences tab.
- **When the cards of one partner disagree**, for example BELFÖLD in one company and IC in another, we do not pick one. The partner keeps what it had and is listed on the Differences tab. The place to correct it is Business Central.
- **A vendor card and a customer card can carry different codes and still agree.** On an outgoing invoice it does not matter whether the buyer is a cash-method or a small-business-exempt taxpayer: it counts as a domestic partner. So PÉNZFORG or AAM on the vendor card beside BELFÖLD on the customer card is not a difference. The partner takes the group of the vendor card, and its outgoing invoices are computed as domestic.
- **A code with no mapping** writes nothing, and the group of the partner can still be set by hand.
- **A partner without a card** keeps the group you set.

At the bottom of **Master data → Business Central → Differences**, the **Partner VAT groups** section appears only when it has something to show: cards with different groups (between companies, or between the vendor card and the customer card), codes without a mapping, and linked cards that carry no group.

On an invoice the partner VAT group is decided in this order: what somebody chose by hand on that invoice, then the group of the partner, then what we infer from the country of the tax number on an invoice that came from NAV. A choice made on an invoice affects that one invoice only, never the group of the partner. On an outgoing invoice a cash-method or small-business-exempt partner counts as domestic, unless somebody chose otherwise on that invoice.

## Pushing Partners as Vendors

**Master data → Business Central → Push** creates vendor cards in Business Central from your partners. This is one of the places AI Finance Team writes into your accounting system; the others are sending invoices (with their PDF), sending bank lines and creating units of measure, below. It needs three things:

- the write side switched on for the workspace, which only we can do;
- the permission "Push into the accounting system", which the accountant admin holds by default;
- **posting groups for each paired company**, which the screen asks for first and pre-fills from your own vendor cards.

You choose the partners, then the companies each one should be created in, and review the list before it runs. At most 200 partner and company pairs go in one run. Afterwards each row says what happened: **Created**; **Already there**, when a card with the same tax number and a matching name exists and is linked instead; **Refused**, when a card with the same tax number has a different name; **Unknown**, when Business Central's answer was lost (the next run looks the card up by tax number before creating anything); or **Failed**, with Business Central's own words.

In a partner's row, the **No card in** cell names the company the card would go to. With several companies chosen it shows the first and how many more (for example "+2"), and all of them on hover.

Whether a partner already has a card in a company is decided by the tax number alone. If its card in Business Central has no tax number, the partner looks missing although its card is there, and pushing it would create a second card. So when that company holds a card whose name starts with the same two words as the partner's (Kft., Zrt., e.v., Dr. and the like do not count), the cell shows an amber warning mark, and the opened list shows the card's number, name and tax number under the company, for example "Card with a similar name: S0043 Dr. Minta Ödön, no tax number". After **Review** these partners are also listed above the table, in an amber box. If that card is the partner's, do not create another: untick that company and enter the tax number on the card in Business Central. After the next refresh of that company (once a day on its own, or at once with **Refresh all** on the Differences tab) the partner leaves the list. The warning holds nothing back: if the card with the similar name is not the partner's, for example a separate card per contract, creating the card works as before.

Your own companies are on the list like any partner (they carry the **Own company** badge on the Partners page): a group company that supplies a sister company needs its card there too. A company is never offered for its own Business Central, because a company is not its own supplier.

A vendor number that comes back is shown on the partner's page under its AI Finance Team code, one line per company. That number belongs to a company in Business Central, so do not type it into the partner's own code field.

## Sending Invoices

**Exports → Business Central** is where expense invoices go up, as draft purchase invoices. It is a different screen from the vendor push, under a different menu, behind a different permission.

The top half is a read-only funnel: how many invoices are **ready to send**, how many are **in the period**, and what stops the rest. Each invoice is counted on one line only, the first that applies. The first line is a decision, not a problem: **Not to be booked (the accountant's decision)** holds the invoices the accountant set aside on the **Bookkeeping** queue (see [Filing and Bookkeeping Approval](/filing-and-bookkeeping-approval)). They are never sent, and there is nothing to fix. After it come the fourteen things that can stop an invoice: no company on the invoice, the company not paired, the company not read yet, the partner not verified, no vendor card for that partner in that company, the partner's vendor card blocked in Business Central, a line with no category, the category's ledger account missing from that company, **a line's unit has no Business Central code** (see Units of Measure below), a line with no product or service group or no VAT rate (filled in on the invoice's **Lines & VAT** tab), **a line whose group and rate have no Business Central VAT group mapped** (see VAT Mapping above), no invoice number or delivery date, **bookkeeping approval missing** (see [Filing and Bookkeeping Approval](/filing-and-bookkeeping-approval)), and **the document is a credit note**. Business Central keeps a credit note (a document with a negative total) as a separate document, a purchase credit memo, and only purchase invoices can be sent from here for now. So it waits here, and until then it can be entered in Business Central by hand. A line opens to the invoices behind it, and each one links to its invoice, on the **Lines & VAT** tab when a line is the problem. Where the fix is on another screen, the line also has a **Fix it here** link that takes you there. Two lines, **No company on the invoice** and **A line has no category**, open a drawer from their **Fix** button instead. The partner line has its own **Verify the partners holding it back** button. Four lines have no link: **Not to be booked** and the credit note, where there is nothing to fix, and the missing group or VAT rate and the missing invoice number or delivery date, which are fixed on the invoice itself. For the lines of a company that deducts no VAT two more can stop the invoice: see [A Company That Deducts No VAT](/entity-no-vat-deduction). One more is a warning rather than a blocker: a currency that company does not hold.

Only expense invoices are considered, and never a duplicate or an archived one. **The invoice's own PDF goes with it** (see The PDF below), and a missing PDF never blocks a send.

Every send is recorded on the invoice as a handover. Where the workspace's accounting software is Business Central (see [Ledger](/ledger)), a sent invoice reads **Handed over**, and one changed afterwards **Changed after the handover**. Business Central does not tell AI Finance Team when a draft is posted; when **Send again to Business Central** finds it posted, the invoice reads **Confirmed**, with Business Central's status on its **Approvals** card.

### VAT Posting Setup in Business Central

Check one setting in every paired Business Central company before the first send. When an invoice line arrives, Business Central first works out the VAT product posting group itself, from the default on the line's ledger account, and looks it up in its VAT Posting Setup together with the vendor's VAT business posting group. Only then does it take the tax group code AI Finance Team sends on the line (the one from your VAT Mapping). A ledger account with no default counts as an empty VAT product posting group. If the VAT Posting Setup has no row for the vendor's VAT business posting group with an empty product group, Business Central refuses the line, even though the line carries a tax group code. The header is already up by then, so a draft without its lines is left behind (see below).

What prevents it: in the VAT Posting Setup, add a row for each VAT business posting group with the VAT product posting group left empty, and set the row to **Blocked**. Business Central accepts the line although the row is blocked, and the tax group code from AI Finance Team replaces the empty group on it. The block is there so that nothing is posted on the empty pair itself. The other way is to give every ledger account that invoices go to a default VAT product posting group whose pair exists in the setup. Which one suits your books is for your accountant or your Business Central partner to decide.

### What to know before you press Send

- **At most 200 invoices go per press.**
- **The send runs in the background.** Pressing **Send** returns at once, and a block above the list shows the progress: a bar, the counts, and one row per invoice. You can close the page, the send keeps going. The **Uploads** panel in the bottom-right corner shows it on every page of the workspace.
- **One send runs at a time in a workspace.** While it runs, **Send** is disabled and says why, and the invoices waiting in it leave the list, so nothing is picked twice. Under **Invoices ready to send** a line says how many of them are being sent right now.
- **Stop** stops the invoices that have not started. The one being sent finishes.
- **The send stops after three failures in a row** and marks the rest *Not sent*, so one broken setting cannot burn the whole batch.
- Each invoice shows its state: **Queued**, **Sending**, **Created**, **Failed**, **Incomplete draft**, **We do not know** or **Not sent**. A failed row shows Business Central's own words, and a row that was not sent says why.
- A finished send stays on the screen, also after a reload, until you press **Close**. When it held more than eight invoices, it lists the ones that still need you (failed, incomplete draft, we do not know, not sent, or a PDF that did not upload), and the button under the list ("Show all …") opens the rest. Once a pending send has been resolved, its row shows what the check found: **Created** with the Business Central number, or **Failed** with a note that it can be sent again. A line under the result says how many invoices can be sent again from the list below.
- **The draft's posting date is the invoice's own posting date in AI Finance Team**, the **Posting date** you see on the invoice. By default that is the delivery date (**Fulfillment date** on the invoice); if you changed it on the invoice, the changed date is what Business Central gets. An invoice that never received a posting date goes with its delivery date.
- **Each line goes up with the quantity and unit price printed on the invoice** when the two give the line's net amount to the cent, for example 102 hours at 15,000 Ft. Otherwise the line goes up as quantity 1 with its net amount as the unit price, as before, so the amount booked is the same either way. The line also carries its unit of measure, translated to the code that Business Central company has (DB, ÓRA, HÓ, KG and so on). **A line is never sent with an empty unit.** If a line goes up with its own quantity and that company has no code for its unit, the invoice waits until you map the unit or correct the line (see Units of Measure below). A line that goes up as quantity 1 carries no unit, so it never waits for one. Under each invoice in the list, **Lines:** shows how its lines will go up.
- **A partner with several cards in one company**: see Which card an invoice is booked to below.

Each invoice is checked again at the moment it is sent, with the permissions of the person who pressed **Send**. An invoice that became blocked while it waited is not sent, and its row names what blocks it.

**"We do not know" is the one that matters.** It means the request went and the answer did not come back. Do not create the invoice again by hand: Business Central does **not** refuse a second purchase invoice carrying the same supplier invoice number, so a second one leaves two drafts for one invoice. AI Finance Team does not send it again either until it is resolved. Use **Resolve interrupted sends** under **Troubleshooting**, which asks Business Central what actually happened and sends nothing.

**A draft that went up without its lines is not counted as sent.** Business Central takes an invoice in two steps, the header first and then each line. It can accept the header and refuse a line, for example when its VAT Posting Setup has no row for the line's VAT groups (see VAT Posting Setup in Business Central above). That leaves a draft carrying the supplier's invoice number with fewer lines than the invoice, often none. The send itself already says so: the invoice's row reads **Incomplete draft**, names the draft, says which line Business Central refused and how many of the invoice's lines are on the draft, and lists the steps in order. When the cause is a missing VAT Posting Setup row, the row also names the company and the VAT business posting group. **Resolve interrupted sends** counts the lines of the unposted draft it finds. When lines are missing, the row is red: it names the draft's number, says how many of the invoice's lines are on it, and shows Business Central's own refusal. The invoice stays pending and nothing is deleted. To finish, correct in Business Central what it refused, delete the draft (by hand in Business Central, or with **Look for drafts left behind** and then **Delete from Business Central**), press **Resolve interrupted sends** again, and send the invoice again. A posted document always counts as sent, whatever its lines. A draft you completed by hand in Business Central with fewer lines than the invoice shows the same red row until you post it.

An invoice already up there leaves the sendable count, and the screen says how many of your selection are already in Business Central.

### Which card an invoice is booked to

Some partners have several cards in one Business Central company: one per leasing contract or loan, or an old and a new card after a change of name. This happens with vendor and customer cards alike. An expense invoice is booked to a vendor card, an income invoice to a customer card. AI Finance Team picks the card in this order:

1. **The card chosen on the invoice.** On the invoice’s **Overview**, the **Business Central partner** section lists the partner’s usable cards in the invoice’s company with their Business Central names, which usually carry the contract number. The control says where the card comes from: **By default**, **As for the partner** (the lowest card number decided) or **Set by hand for this invoice**. With a single usable card the section names it, with its name, and has no control. With none it says **A new partner has to be created in Business Central**. If the chosen card is blocked in Business Central later, an expense invoice waits under **The partner’s vendor card is blocked in Business Central**. It is never booked to another card instead.
2. **The partner’s default card.** On the partner’s page, in the **Business Central** section, **Make default** under a card makes it the default in that company, and **Remove default** clears it. Vendor cards and customer cards each have their own default.
3. **Otherwise the lowest card number** Business Central accepts, a card linked to the partner first.

The partner’s page marks the card in use: **Default** when someone chose it, **In use** when the lowest number decided. The short list under **Partner ID** shows each card’s Business Central name too.

A vendor payment on the **Bank lines** tab goes to the card its invoice went to in Business Central, so the payment meets its invoice. A customer receipt goes to the customer card chosen on its income invoice, else to the default, else to the lowest number. AI Finance Team sends no outgoing invoice to Business Central, so the card chosen on an income invoice counts when its receipt is sent. A different card chosen on an expense invoice that is already in Business Central counts only when you send it again.

Choosing a card, on the invoice or on the partner, needs the permission to run exports.

### Sending an invoice again

An invoice that is already in Business Central can be sent again from its own page: on the **Overview** tab, in the **Business Central** section, the **Send again to Business Central** button. It is for an invoice that was corrected here after it was sent, for example a line's category or VAT code, and so reads **Changed after the handover**. Pressing it sends nothing yet: AI Finance Team first asks Business Central what happened to the document it created, and does only what the answer allows. If the document is still a **draft**, it is deleted and a new one is created from the invoice as it is now (with the AIFT id as its number when that setting is on); anything edited by hand on that draft in Business Central is lost, and the confirmation says so. If it has been **posted**, nothing is sent: a posted invoice is corrected in Business Central, with a credit memo or a correction. If it is **no longer there**, the invoice goes up as new. If **several** documents carry the supplier's invoice number, or Business Central cannot be reached, nothing changes and the screen says why. The retired document stays in the invoice's **History** tab, so two "sent" rows on one invoice explain themselves, and the PDF goes up again with the new document.

### The PDF

The invoice's own PDF goes up as an attachment of the draft, in its **Attachments** box. Business Central copies it to the posted invoice when you post.

- Each row of the list says whether a PDF goes with it: **PDF** or **No PDF**. An invoice that came from NAV has no file. That is not an error, and it never blocks a send.
- Once the invoice is created, its row shows the PDF: **Uploading the PDF**, then **PDF attached**, or **The PDF did not upload.** with Business Central's own words and a **Send the PDF again** button.
- Only the invoice's own PDF goes. Other documents attached to the invoice in AI Finance Team stay there.
- If the content of a file fails to upload, we delete the empty attachment row we have just made, because Business Central refuses to post an invoice that carries an empty attachment.
- When invoices sent earlier have no PDF in Business Central, a line above the list says how many, with an **Add the PDFs (N)** button. It asks first, then uploads them in the background. A PDF that reaches an invoice after it was sent goes up only this way: nothing uploads by itself.

### The maintenance buttons

Eleven buttons sit under the list, in the **Troubleshooting** section, in two groups, **Check** and **Resolve**, which is closed until you open it. The section is there even when nothing is sendable, because a stray draft in the client's ledger is invisible to anyone without a Business Central login.

- **Probe** creates a real throwaway draft from a real invoice and deletes it again. It answers whether this tenant accepts a duplicate supplier invoice number, and shows what we sent beside what Business Central stored. It also puts the invoice's PDF on the throwaway draft, to show whether this tenant takes attachments. Its source is the invoice you tick in the list (exactly one); with none ticked it uses the first invoice ready to send in the workspace, or one already sent. A line under the button names the source, so tick one whose lines carry what you want to test.
- **What BC exposes about a vendor** reads a vendor card and writes nothing.
- **What BC holds about customers and outgoing invoices** reads the customer cards and where each outgoing invoice stands in Business Central, and writes nothing.
- **How BC takes a purchase credit memo** reads how this Business Central takes a purchase credit memo (the fields of the document and of its lines, the pages it publishes, the number series it would use) and the credit memos already there. It also looks up where each credit note AI Finance Team holds stands in Business Central: as a credit memo, as a purchase invoice, or nowhere. It looks up at most five credit notes per company, oldest first, and writes nothing.
- **Where BC keeps the VAT date of a draft** lists the published pages and their fields whose name mentions VAT and a date, what the most recently sent document holds in each, that invoice's dates in AI Finance Team, and what General Ledger Setup says about the VAT date. It writes nothing.
- **Numbering trial (made-up invoice)** creates one made-up purchase invoice draft numbered AIFT-TESZT-…, clears its posting number series, adds a line and deletes it again. It shows whether this Business Central accepts our own number as the invoice number (see The AIFT Id as the Invoice Number below). It asks for confirmation first.
- **VAT date write probe (made-up invoice)** creates one made-up purchase invoice draft numbered AIFT-TESZT-…, sets its VAT date once, reads the header and the lines back, and deletes the draft again. It shows whether this Business Central lets us set the VAT date of a draft, and whether the lines follow it. Nothing is posted. It asks for confirmation first.
- **VAT date write probe, with the original VAT date (made-up invoice)** does the same, plus one more write on the made-up draft: it sets the original VAT date to a third date and reads it back. Press it only when we ask you to. It asks for confirmation first.
- **Bank journal probe (made-up lines)** creates a journal batch named AIFTSZONDA and writes four made-up lines of 1 into it (two G/L lines, a supplier payment and a customer receipt applied to an open invoice), reads each one back, then deletes the lines and the batch. Nothing is posted. It asks for confirmation first.
- **Resolve interrupted sends** asks what happened to a send whose answer was lost. It neither sends nor deletes. A draft it finds without all of its lines is shown in red and is not recorded as sent.
- **Look for drafts left behind** finds drafts we recorded but never linked. Deleting them is a separate, red **Delete from Business Central** button: the check and the delete are two steps on purpose.

## Sending Bank Lines

The **Exports → Business Central → Bank lines** tab sends bank lines into the Business Central general journal as **unposted lines**. The bookkeeper posts them, in Business Central.

First, on **Master data → Business Central → Bank accounts**, set:

- the **journal**, per bank account, in the **Journal** column of its row: the journal template and the batch, picked from the list AI Finance Team reads from that account's company in Business Central (every batch of every template; refresh it on Master data → Business Central). Business Central keeps batches per company, so there is no workspace-wide default: an account without a journal sends nothing. We recommend a batch of AI Finance Team's own, so its lines never mix with lines typed by hand. If Business Central does not publish the batch list for a company, the two fields can be typed;
- for each bank account, the **Business Central bank account** its lines balance against. When the IBAN matches we propose it, but it only takes effect once you save it;
- for each bank account, the **start date**. Lines before it are not sent, because the bookkeeping has recorded them already. **Without a start date no line of that account is sent.** Pick the day after the last day the bank was booked by hand.

Three kinds of line go:

- a **no-invoice** line (a bank fee, for example) as a G/L line on its category's account number, **without VAT**;
- a line matched to an expense invoice as a **vendor payment**, applied to that invoice;
- a line matched to a sales invoice as a **customer receipt**, applied to that invoice.

The posting date is the value date and the document number is AI Finance Team's id (for example AH2-TXN-2026-0412). The external document number is left empty, because none of the sources carries a statement number today. The amount goes in its original currency; Business Central converts it with its own exchange rates.

Not sent, and the tab says why:

- a line that is neither matched to an invoice nor marked "No invoice needed";
- a payment whose invoice is not posted in Business Central yet, because a payment cannot be applied to a draft;
- a payment whose invoice is already paid in Business Central: someone has recorded it already, and it would go in twice;
- a single transfer that pays several invoices: for now this is booked in Business Central by hand;
- a line that pays another company's invoice, from a cash pool for example;
- a vendor payment to a vendor card that is blocked for payments in Business Central (blocked entirely or for payments);
- a customer receipt to a customer card that is blocked in Business Central.

### Before You Send

- **One press sends at most 500 lines**, in the background, with the same bar and list as the invoice send. One send runs in a workspace at a time, invoices or bank lines.
- **A payment only goes to a posted, open invoice.** Before sending, the tab asks Business Central (read only) whether each payment's invoice is posted and not yet paid. When someone who may send opens the page, it asks for the payments that are due an answer, and **Refresh** asks again, for example after you have posted the invoices. Until there is such an answer the payment does not count as sendable, and the **What holds the rest back** list says why: not posted yet, not found (the line shows which Business Central partner was searched), already paid, several open invoices match, or not asked yet.
- **The Send button in the page title** is on both tabs and sends everything sendable in the period and company set on the page, without picking rows: **Everything**, **Invoices only** or **Bank lines only**. With everything it is one send, invoices first, then bank lines. A payment whose invoice goes in the same send waits until the invoice is posted in Business Central, and goes with the next send.
- **Nothing goes twice.** Business Central does not refuse a second identical payment, so before every write we ask it whether a line with the same AI Finance Team id is already in that bank account's journal batch or among the posted entries. If it is, we do not write it again.
- **Every line written is read back.** If Business Central recorded it differently from what we sent (a VAT group on a G/L line, for example), the line is deleted and its row says why.
- A line that went is marked **"In Business Central already"** and no longer counts as sendable.
- **We do not know** means here too that the request went out and no answer came back. Do not book the line by hand. Such a line is not sent again until it is resolved: later sends skip it and say that an earlier send lost its answer. Use **Resolve interrupted sends** under **Troubleshooting** on the **Invoices** tab. It asks Business Central whether the line is in the journal batch or already posted, and it sends nothing. If the line is there, it is recorded as sent. If it is nowhere, it can be sent again.

## Payment Methods

**Master data → Business Central → Payment methods** maps each AI Finance Team payment method (transfer, cash, card, voucher, other) onto one Business Central code, as a workspace default or per company. A code is proposed only where exactly one Business Central code reads as that method.

The mapping is applied to the draft immediately after it is created, because the interface has no payment-method field on the document itself. **A missing mapping is not an error** and never blocks a send. An individual invoice can override the mapping on its own payment tab, and the send list shows the code each invoice will go up with.

## Units of Measure

**Master data → Business Central → Units** lists every unit found on the invoices that can be sent, with the Business Central code it goes up as. Most units match by themselves: the unit is the code, the code's name, or a common spelling of it (darab and PIECE are DB, óra and HOUR are ÓRA). The units that hold invoices are at the top, with the number of lines and invoices waiting on them. Click the number to see the invoices held, each with a link to its **Lines & VAT** tab. Every row also names the companies that lack a code. On this page every company carries the name it has in AI Finance Team, not its name in Business Central.

For a unit with no match, choose a code from the list of codes your companies have. **One choice applies to every line with that unit**, now and later, in every company that has the code. A company that does not have the chosen code is named on the row. **OWN** (the seller's own unit, with no text) cannot be mapped, because it means something different on every invoice, and neither can a unit that is only punctuation (a full stop, for example): correct such a line on the invoice.

**Create in Business Central.** When a unit has no code in a company, the row's **Create in Business Central** button creates the unit in Business Central, much like pushing partners, so you do not have to enter it there by hand. In the dialog you fill in **Code** (at most 10 characters) and **Name** and tick the companies: every paired company that lacks the code is ticked to start with. In each company we first look whether the code is already there; what is there is never rewritten. The new unit shows on the page at once, and the invoices waiting on it can be sent unless something else holds them. If the code you enter is not the unit on the invoice, the mapping is saved too. The **Units in Business Central** table also shows which code each company lacks, and **Create in these companies** creates it from there. A unit that no company has yet is created with **New unit** above the table: you type the code, and every paired company is ticked to start with. The units created from here, and what Business Central refused (in its own words), are listed under **Created in Business Central**. The button needs the “Push into the accounting system” permission and writing into Business Central switched on for the workspace, and it appears only once AI Finance Team has switched this option on; until then the page says so in one line.

On the invoice's **Lines & VAT** tab, each line shows what it goes up as: the quantity, the unit price and the unit code, or quantity 1 with the net amount and the reason. Someone who may edit invoices can press **Correct quantity and unit** and change the **Quantity**, the **Unit** and the **Unit price** together. The net amount never changes, so quantity times unit price must give the net to the cent, and the form shows whether it does before you save. The previous values stay in the invoice's history. An invoice already posted in Business Central cannot be corrected here; a draft that was already sent goes up corrected when you send it again.

## Posting Description

In the same step as the payment method, each draft also gets its **Posting Description**: the invoice summary from AI Finance Team, so the vendor ledger entry and the general ledger show what the invoice is about instead of just "Invoice" and a number. Business Central allows 100 characters there. A longer summary is cut at the last whole word and ends with "…". The text keeps the invoice's own language, and no AI is used to shorten it.

The send list shows the text under each invoice before you send it. An invoice without a summary keeps Business Central's own default text, and that never blocks a send. The draft stays editable in Business Central, so you can change the text before you post.

Whether the text also appears on the expense lines of the general ledger depends on a Business Central setting: with **Copy Line Descr. to G/L Entry** switched on in Purchases & Payables Setup, those lines carry each invoice line's own text instead, and the Posting Description stays on the vendor side.

## The AIFT Id as the Invoice Number

A switch under **Settings → Numbering** makes invoices sent from AI Finance Team take their AIFT id as their number in Business Central (for example AH2-INV-2026-0295), instead of the next number of the company's own series. It becomes the draft's number and, once posted, the posted invoice's number too. Invoices typed into Business Central keep their own numbering.

Two things to know first:

- **The numbering will have gaps, and two formats run side by side.** The AIFT sequence runs across every company in the workspace and across sales and purchase invoices, so within one company the AIFT-numbered invoices are not consecutive. The year is the invoice's issue year.
- **The AIFT id does not replace the supplier's invoice number.** That still goes into the vendor invoice number field.

To keep the number after posting, we clear the posted-invoice number series on the draft. Business Central then carries the draft's number over to the posted invoice.

### What has to be set in Business Central

The switch works only if AI Finance Team can read which number series purchase invoices use, and whether it accepts manual numbers. For that, the Business Central administrator publishes two pages as web services, on the **Web Services** page (Alt+Q):

| Object Type | Object ID | Service Name | Published |
|---|---|---|---|
| Page | 460 | PurchasesPayablesSetup | ticked |
| Page | 456 | NoSeries | ticked |

Both are read only. On top of that, **Manual Nos.** must be switched on for the purchase invoice number series (**Invoice Nos.**). Invoices typed into Business Central still get their numbers automatically.

**Copy these steps** on the Numbering page puts these instructions on your clipboard, ready to forward to the administrator.

### Checking and refusing

When you switch it on, AI Finance Team checks every paired company at once and shows, per company: **Ready**, **Not ready** or **Cannot check**. For the last two it also shows the Business Central step that fixes it.

Every send checks again. **If a company is not ready, or cannot be checked, its invoices are not sent**, and the send list says why. Nothing goes out with a Business Central number instead, because an invoice already sent cannot be renumbered.

The switch needs the "Push into the accounting system" permission.

## Who Can Do What

Connecting, disconnecting and pairing need the permission "Manage connections", held by default by the accountant admin and the finance lead. A custom role or an exception can change that. Everyone else sees the status and cannot change it.

Sending anything into Business Central needs **two** permissions: "Run accounting exports" to reach the send screen at all, which the accountant admin and the accountant hold, and "Push into the accounting system" to actually send, which only the accountant admin holds. An accountant without the second one can open the screen and read it, but the send buttons and **Refresh** are not shown, and the invoice check says that someone with the send permission can ask. Saving the **Bank accounts** page needs the "Edit master data" permission.

## Disconnecting

**Disconnect** on **Settings → Business Central Connection**. The confirmation says what goes: the pairings, the copied master data and the VAT mappings are deleted. The **payment method mapping goes too**, which the dialog does not mention. Your companies, categories, partners, the links to vendor cards, the bank account settings (Business Central bank account, start date, journal) and the record of which invoices and bank lines were sent are all kept.

Reconnecting afterwards needs **both approvals again**, and every company paired again. Your Microsoft administrator can also revoke the approval in your own Microsoft 365 without telling us.
