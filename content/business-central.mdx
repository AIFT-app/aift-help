# Connecting Business Central

AI Finance Team can read the companies in your Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central. This page explains what has to be approved, and by whom.

Setting it up needs **two separate approvals**, and they are usually given by two different people. Granting one does not grant the other. That is the single thing most worth understanding before you start.

---

## The two approvals

**1. Microsoft approval.** Someone with **Global Administrator** rights in your Microsoft 365 approves AI Finance Team once. This is usually your IT administrator or your IT provider. It takes about a minute and happens on a Microsoft page, not ours.

**2. Business Central registration.** Someone with **SUPER** rights inside Business Central adds our application there and gives it a permission set. This is often the accountant rather than the IT administrator.

There is no way to automate the second one. Business Central has no interface for it, so a person has to do it by hand. Approval alone leaves the connection refusing every request, which is why AI Finance Team tells the two states apart instead of showing one generic "not connected".

---

## Step 1: send the approval link

In AI Finance Team, open **Settings → Business Central** and choose **Create the approval link**.

You will get a link and a ready-written message. Send both to whoever administers your Microsoft 365. The link expires after 7 days, and you can create a new one at any time. Creating a new one stops the previous one working.

Two things to expect:

- Microsoft will warn that **the publisher is not verified**. That is normal for this application, and it does not stop an administrator from approving. It stops ordinary users from approving, which is not what happens here.
- The administrator sees a page confirming the approval landed. It does not mention your company by name, because anyone holding that link can open it.

The permission granted is read and write on the Business Central API. It does not allow creating or deleting environments.

---

## Step 2: register the application in Business Central

Once Microsoft approval lands, AI Finance Team shows an **Application ID** and these steps. Send them to whoever holds SUPER rights in Business Central.

1. In Business Central, press **Alt+Q** and search for **Microsoft Entra Applications**.
2. Choose **New** and paste in the Application ID.
3. Set the description to **AI Finance Team integration**.
4. Set **State** to **Enabled**.
5. Add the permission set **D365 BUS FULL ACCESS**.
6. Leave the **Company** field on that line **empty**.

Three things go wrong here more often than anything else:

- **Naming a company on that line** silently limits AI Finance Team to that one company. Everything appears to work, and the other companies simply never show up. Leave it empty.
- **Using D365 AUTOMATION** instead. The connection succeeds and then returns nothing useful, because that permission set covers administrative APIs rather than business data.
- **Using SUPER.** It works and grants far more than the integration needs. Do not.

The registration is **per environment**. Doing it in Production does not cover a Sandbox; each environment needs its own.

---

## What the statuses mean

| Status | What it means | Who acts next |
|---|---|---|
| Not connected | No approval link has been created yet. | You. |
| Waiting for Microsoft approval | The link exists but nobody has approved it. | Your Microsoft administrator. |
| Waiting for Business Central registration | Microsoft approved us and our sign-in works. Business Central is still refusing, so step 2 is not done. | Your Business Central administrator. |
| Connected | We can read your companies. | Nobody. |
| Error | Something specific went wrong. The page shows the code. | Depends on the code. |

If the status says the environment could not be found, check the environment name in your own Business Central address. It is **Production** for most customers.

---

## What AI Finance Team reads

At this stage, only the **list of companies**: their names and identifiers. Nothing is written back to Business Central, and no invoice, transaction or balance is read yet.

Connecting company data to the companies in AI Finance Team is a separate step, and you choose which Business Central companies to connect. Companies you do not connect are simply left alone.

---

## Pairing Business Central companies with AI Finance Team companies

Once the connection works, the Business Central page lists every company in your Business Central. Each Business Central company can be paired with one company in AI Finance Team, and each AI Finance Team company with one Business Central company.

There are two ways to pair:

- **Create a new company from a Business Central company.** AI Finance Team takes the name, the tax number, the country and the address from Business Central. You can tick several companies and create them all in one go.
- **Pair with a company you already have.** Use this when the company is already in AI Finance Team.

AI Finance Team suggests a match where it can. A suggestion based on the **tax number** is reliable. A suggestion based on the **name** is only a suggestion, so check it before you accept it.

### One company per tax number

A workspace holds each company once. If a Business Central company's tax number already belongs to a company in AI Finance Team, the row offers **Pair** only, never **Create company**. If that company is already paired with another Business Central company (a test copy of the same firm, for example), the row says which one, and you unpair that first if this is the right one.

### Where you can start

There are two places to create a company from a Business Central company.

- **Master data ▸ Business Central ▸ Companies.** Every company that is not connected yet has a **Create company** button, and a checkbox to create several at once.
- **Master data ▸ Companies.** When your workspace has a working connection and some companies are still unconnected, a **Create companies from Business Central** button sits next to **Add company**. It lists only the companies that are not connected yet, and marks the ones that already exist in AI Finance Team.

Both do the same thing and both connect the Business Central company to the company they create.

Creating one at a time shows you the legal name, country and tax number first, and lets you correct the name before the company exists. Creating several at once takes the values from Business Central as they stand.

### What gets written, and what does not

When you pair with an existing company, you can copy the address and the tax number across from Business Central. **Only empty fields can be filled.** A value that is already in AI Finance Team is never overwritten, whatever you tick. To change a value you already have, edit the company itself.

Nothing is ever written back to Business Central.

### Companies you cannot pair

A Business Central company whose tax number is a **VAT group identifier**, that is a group registration rather than a company, cannot become a company in AI Finance Team. AI Finance Team says so on the row. Leave it unpaired. It is a filing arrangement, not a business.

### Warnings you may see

- **Different currency.** Business Central keeps that company's books in a currency other than your workspace's base currency. This is a warning only. It never stops you pairing.
- **Different tax numbers.** The Business Central company and the company you are pairing it with state different tax numbers. Check which one is right before you accept.

### Undoing a pair

Unpair from the same page, or from the company's own page. Unpairing removes the connection and nothing else. The company stays exactly as it is, and nothing changes in Business Central.

## Accounts as categories

Business Central cannot create a ledger account through its API, so the client's chart of accounts stays theirs. To let an AIFT category name a ledger account, bring the accounts into AIFT as categories.

Open **Master data > Accounting categories** and choose **Import accounts from Business Central**. The button is on both the **Invoice** and the **Cash** tab.

At the top, **Create as** decides what each ticked account becomes:

- **Invoice categories**: for invoice lines.
- **Cash categories**: for bank movements.
- **Both**: one invoice category and one cash category with the same account number. They stay two separate categories, so renaming one does not rename the other.

The list shows the accounts that do not have a category of the chosen kind yet. Tick the ones you want, check the direction, then choose **Create**. The categories are created straight away. The next screen shows what was created and which accounts were skipped, with the reason for each.

**The account number is what identifies the category.** Two accounts with different numbers become two categories even when they have the same name. An account number that already has a category of that kind is skipped and left as it is.

### Copying invoice categories to cash categories

On the **Cash** tab, **Copy from invoice categories** creates a cash category for every invoice category the cash side does not have yet, with the same account number, name and direction. It shows the counts first and changes nothing that already exists. Built-in categories are not copied, because the cash side has its own.

---

## Who can set this up

A workspace administrator or the client owner can connect and disconnect. Everyone else in the workspace can see the status but cannot change it.

You can disconnect at any time from the same page. Your Microsoft administrator can also revoke the approval in your own Microsoft 365 without asking us.
